# Make Luna Great Again

We were once the holy grail of the Cosmos chain.
I know it’s painful for many of us to think back to that day when everything started to collapse, and our beloved Luna could no longer be saved. Do Kwon had a beautiful vision—one that many of us shared: _a decentralized economy needs a decentralized stablecoin.

Since then, TFL has made it clear that they won’t take the risk of creating a new stablecoin on Luna. But now that the chain is in the hands of the community, the power to make such decisions lies with us.
I’m not a developer myself, but I believe that if all the talented people still active in this community come together—both the devs and everyone else—we can create something great again.


# First Steps: Borrow and Lending Protocol

To start, we need a borrow-and-lending protocol. This could either be built from scratch and funded by the community pool or by bringing an existing dApp back to Luna. For example, Neptune previously received funding from us but eventually moved to Injective to launch their product. Perhaps we could explore opportunities there.


# A Stablecoin: Learning from Past Mistakes

I firmly believe we should attempt to create a stablecoin again—but this time, learn from the mistakes we made with UST.
What made UST and Luna so unique was the burning mechanism itself. Perhaps we can come up with a refined version of this idea, enabling Luna’s “oven” to burn tokens once more in a more sustainable way.
Since then, the landscape has evolved. For instance, we now have AI at our disposal, which opens up new possibilities:

- **AI-Driven Risk Management:** Implement AI systems to assess market risks and optimize stabilization mechanisms in real time.
- **Full Reserve Backing:** Build a reserve fund that is diverse and liquid, combining various assets like fiat, crypto, and 
commodities.(using the community pool)
- **Incremental Decentralization:** Begin with a semi-centralized model to establish trust, then transition gradually to full decentralization.
- **Sustainable Reward Structures:** Design a rewards system that builds trust without relying on unsustainable exponential growth (like Luna did previously).
- **Avoid Ponzi-like Structures:** Eliminate models where new inflows are needed to sustain prices (e.g., avoid repeating the flaws of Anchor Protocol).

If the stablecoin’s price drops below its peg, a portion of Luna tokens could still be burned to reduce supply and restore the peg—but this time combined with reserve-backed stabilization to prevent a collapse.

**Let’s Build Something Unique Together**

These are just a few basic ideas. I’m confident that, with collective brainstorming and effort, we can create something truly unique and innovative—something Luna can once again be proud of.
Let’s not forget: we are now the owners of this chain. It’s up to us to steer it toward a brighter future. Let’s rise together.
