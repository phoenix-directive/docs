## Executive Summary

**TFM terminal is a gateway to bring new users to Terra Money and chains launched using Feather.**

TFM is building a pro DeFi trading terminal to bring on-chain traders to Terra.

Virtually every DeFi product caters to TVL, under the assumption liquidity alone attracts traders.

TFM targets the end-user: traders. 

Building tools that traders want ensures creation of a brand and resource mote, where end-users (liquidity consumers) are acquired & retained.

Since June 2022, TFM has shipped multiple products on Terra:

- TFM terminal - trading dashboard for beginners through to pro-traders
- TFM _router_ - our DEX aggregator smart contract incorporating volume splitting and multi-hop routing 
- _Router_ smart contract audited by SCV Security (7 June 2022).
- TFM API - enabling incorporation of TFM DEX aggregation into other dapps. TFL has integrated the TFM _router_ into TerraStation.
- Project ecosystem dashboard (MVP) - Details of all projects building upon Terra blockchain


Nansen data shows we are currently the second most used protocol on Terra, behind Astroport - [https://pro.nansen.ai/multichain/terra](https://pro.nansen.ai/multichain/terra)

**In Development:**

1. Limit orders - _Limit order_ smart contract has been audited by SCV Security.
2. NFT aggregator (MVP) - We have already started work on v2 of our NFT aggregator including UI/UX improvements, numerous analytics for NFT devotees.
3. Asset tracking dashboard - within Terra and across Cosmos.
4. LP dashboard - manage all LP positions from a single interface.
5. Enhanced project ecosystem dashboard
6. Validator staking + profile dashboard



## Funding request

We are requesting $1.2M worth of LUNA to fund continued development, as well as maintenance of all existing products. 

This funding will provide the required development runway of the 6 new products above.

We propose the funding be split into 6 equal installments, with $200K worth of LUNA sent to a multi-sig from community pool once per month.

Multi-sig wallet to require 4 of 7 trusted community members to sign off on transfer to TFM.

Initial $200K worth of LUNA to be transferred upon passing of this proposal. Future disbursements on the same date, once per month.

Prior to each disbursement, once a month TFM shall provide a detailed update on our development progress. Trusted council would review our progress and sign off on the monthly installments.

In total, we are asking for $1.2M worth of LUNA from the community pool to fund the 6 new products listed above and maintain the full TFM product suite.
