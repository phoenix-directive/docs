**Summary**
Treat pre-depeg UST holders who had UST in LPs on terra based dexes with the same dignity as pre-deg aUST holders.

**Motivation**
aUST holders received luna in the genesis airdrop for placing their money in Anchor. Lunatics who held their UST in LPs pre-depeg were doing a service to the chain, yet received nothing in the Genesis airdrop.

**Proposal**
In line with the indexing of LPs for the Pheonix Airdrop, allocate from the community fund, a portion of Luna towards UST holders who held their UST in Luna LPs pre-depeg. 

An example of this would be Luna-UST, LunaX-UST, StLuna-UST LPs on Terraswap, Astroport, Loop etc... dexes that were on the terra chain pre-depeg.
