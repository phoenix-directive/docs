Dear Terra Community Members,

Neptune Finance, now known as the Neptune DeFi Bank, would like to request funding from the community fund to reimburse a smart contract audit conducted by Oak Security. 

At the time the decision was made, the Neptune team was told the audit would be reimbursed if we chose from a select list of auditors. The audit reimbursement program was then closed without notice and we were informed audit reimbursements must now be requested from the community fund.

**Why we chose Oak Security**
The Neptune team received quotes from all the listed auditors and chose Oak Security based on the scope of work, total cost, timeline, reputation, and overall experience with money markets. 

The smart contract audit has helped confirm the security of our code and patch some minor reliability issues providing us with the confidence to let users try the platform. 

**Requested amount**
The reimbursement amount is $45,000 USD or approximately 35,500 LUNA. To account for possible value fluctuations, we would like to request 40,000 LUNA and return any amount that may exceed the requested $45,000 reimbursement. 

There would be no multi-sig created for this proposal as it will be a reimbursement for work that has already been paid and completed. A reimbursement address will be provided for the on-chain proposal. 

**Community fund investment**
The audit is complete and the findings have been addressed. We hope the community sees the benefit to covering this audit as a necessary step to promote an ecosystem of healthy, audited protocols on Terra. [See the full audit report](https://github.com/oak-security/audit-reports/blob/master/Neptune/2023-04-09 Audit Report - Neptune Protocol v1.0.pdf). 

We are confident that investing in Neptune’s security is in the best interest of the Terra community and this reimbursement will help build trust with users and grow the platform in a secure and sustainable way. 

Once the Neptune protocol has completed its beta testing period, the protocol should contribute measurable value back to the Terra community. 

We acknowledge the importance of transparency and community involvement and we are working towards a more open development process. However, we must prioritise the security and stability of our code as we continue to build, monitor, and refine the protocol at this stage of deployment. Eventually, our code will be ready for open-source and we are committed to take that step in the future.

If there are any comments, concerns, or proposed adjustments we can make to the proposal, we welcome your comments below. 

We hope that the community will see the value in this reimbursement and will support this proposal.
