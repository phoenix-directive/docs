# Summary
CrossFi is a soon-to-launch L1 Cosmos/EVM chain focused on crypto banking/payments, DeFi, institutional capital, and enabling permissionless access to a broad range of synthetic assets. We are led by ex-Binance c-suite, unicorn startup leaders, and the banking/TradFi elite.

The chain's architecture is exceptionally similar to Terra's, to the point where dApps could essentially be copied and pasted over. This would provide a supportive home for Terra's builders, who have shown resilience and loyalty despite lacking leadership and support. Coming to CrossFi is advantageous because you will have access to a familiar development environment, support, a clear trajectory, and a grants program totalling $50m USD, which is critical for dApp/chain traction and success.


# Motivation
We believe in lifting each other up, and this is the ethos of the Cosmos ecosystem as a whole. While the outside space is competitive, what matters most to us, and I'm sure to the community leaders at Terra and those who care deeply about the _people_ who have made Terra what it is despite challenges, is that the builders who have developed incredible things should be given every opportunity to succeed. CrossFi presents that second-chance opportunity for Terra builders whose traction and adoption were hindered only by circumstances beyond their control. The reality of a complete Terra ecosystem comeback at this point is both a gamble for ecosystem participants and an uphill battle.


# Proposal
We propose fully porting Terra's ecosystem dApps to CrossFi for all builders and businesses open to it. Technical and potential grant support will be available to all, and everyone will have a second opportunity to lead dApps in an emerging ecosystem. 
For those with organisational influence within the Terra community, as well as interested builders, I would encourage you to reach out to me through the mediums below:

TG: https://t.me/OxYoshi
Email: josh@crossfi.org

Since we are also within the Cosmos ecosystem and deeply empathise with the Terra community's plight, we will prioritise Terra ecosystem builder applications for grant considerations and additional support.
