# Summary:

This is a proposal to add ampROAR, a liquid staking token of ROAR (the governance token of Lion DAO), as an Alliance asset on the Terra blockchain. Holders of ampROAR will be able to stake ampROAR to Terra validators and accrue LUNA staking rewards.

The proposed weight is 0.008 (.72% of the LUNA staking rewards) with a 0% take rate aligned with the request from AllianceDAO NFTs.

By adding ampROAR as an alliance asset, this prop seeks to establish a unique role for Lion DAO in promoting the adoption and evolution of the Alliance module for Terra and its community by demonstrating the ability to form internal alliances on a chain to support active projects that bring value to the chain.

# What is ampROAR?

ampROAR is an autocompounding liquid staking token of ROAR, the Lion DAO governance token, developed by Eris Protocol who are a prominent LST provider across Cosmos but born on Terra. ampROAR was the first example of an LST for a non-native token on both Terra & potentially across Cosmos.

Users deposit ROAR into the Eris vault, Eris then stake the ROAR in Lion DAO and autocompound the daily staking rewards into the LST. Holders of ampROAR can then redeem the ROAR LST via the Eris UI to claim the underlying ROAR with a 7-8 day unbonding period. 

Lion DAO utilize Warp Protocol, another TFL developed app, to distribute ROAR staking rewards daily and once again were a pioneer in their utilization of Warp being only the second protocol to have a recurring job on Warp, after Eris themselves.

To ensure that too much governance power doesn’t lie in the hands of Eris, Eris have implemented ampGovernance for ampROAR, meaning that users can lock their ampROAR for a period of 2 weeks to 2 years in order to have leveraged governance power.

We have also established ampROAR as a collateral asset that can be used as collateral to borrow native USDC on the Ginkou app on Migaloo chain.


# Benefits to the Terra Community of allowing ampROAR as an Alliance asset:

**New LUNA Alliances with Other Cosmos Chains:** Lion DAO will continue to promote the adoption of the Alliance module and the whitelisting of LUNA LSTs as Alliance assets to other Cosmos chains, unlocking more cross-chain yield opportunities for Terra users, similar to the current alliances with Migaloo and Carbon.

**Community Growth & Engagement:** As an active participant in the Terra community, Lion DAO will continue to foster growth and retention of active users on Terra. We have already established that Lion DAO has made a huge contribution to wallet activity on Terra, promoting and showcasing Terra tooling and protocols and distributing our Terra native token into a wide set of hands on Terra and across Cosmos. Through the implementation of ampROAR as an Alliance asset we expect that audience, holder base and transaction volume to continue to grow.

**Redirection of LUNA staking rewards to active network participants:** Due to the huge amount of wallets that qualified for the LUNA v2 airdrop a lot of staking rewards are being distributed to dead wallets or users/entities that are constantly selling off their staking rewards. The distribution of the ROAR token was done in a way to reward only active network participants, therefore giving a high degree of confidence that the vast majority of ROAR holders are active network participants. By diverting a small amount of staking rewards to ampROAR holders, we will ensure that inflationary LUNA is being distributed to this group of active Terra users, rewarding them for remaining active on the Terra network at a time when it would have been easy for them to abandon the chain.

**Increased Brand/Ecosystem Awareness:** By allowing ampROAR as an Alliance asset we will demonstrate that Terra is a chain that continues to support the active projects on the blockchain. Listing ampROAR will be a showcase to other projects, considering building on Terra, that there are multiple avenues the project can take to get direct support from the Terra blockchain and its community. It will also demonstrate the use case of the Alliance module as a tool that can be used to create “internal alliances” between ecosystem projects and the blockchain itself, as has already been demonstrated by Migaloo and is being considered by the Kujira blockchain. Thus continuing to promote adoption of the Alliance module across Cosmos.

**Enterprise DAO/Warp Adoption:** Lion DAO has been fundamental to the growth of Enterprise DAO by demonstrating its features and utility to new communities. By utilizing ampROAR as an Alliance asset, we will continue to encourage Enterprise DAO adoption, which means more transaction volume (and more fees paid) on the Terra blockchain, which benefits all Terra ecosystem participants.
