## Proposal Summary
The purpose of this proposal is to establish a mutually beneficial alliance between Terra and the Galactic Punks community through the use of Galactic Punk NFTs. 

The Galactic DAO is proposing Galactic Punk NFTs are added as Alliance Assets with a Reward Rate of 0.005, which will result in less than half a percent (< 0.5%) of LUNA staking rewards being redirected towards Galactic Punks staked into the Alliance module. This reward rate is up for debate, and we encourage Terra ecosystem participants to provide feedback. 

## Alliance Benefits
The benefits of this alliance to LUNA stakers and the Terra community as a whole are significant. 

First, this partnership will demonstrate the adaptability and flexibility of the Alliance module, providing a model for future economic collaborations amongst various key communities and players, both fungible and non-fungible. This would be a first across all existing eco-systems, providing a new form of financial incentive model for NFTs participating in an ecosystem. 

Additionally, with 2,292 holders (at the time of writing) on Terra, an alliance with the Galactic Punk community will promote Alliance across social media channels and increase on-chain activity within Terra. As the Galactic Punks collection was built on Terra and now has members distributed throughout the Cosmos as well as other L1s including Solana and Ethereum. This alliance presents an opportunity to spread the Alliance message far and wide. 

Moreover, this partnership creates a massive incentive for both Terra Classic GP holders and Terra GP holders to migrate and stake their Galactic Punks into the Alliance module. This migration will further promote the Alliance module and increase on-chain activity in Terra.

We believe the collection should evolve with the alliance module to maintain its unique position as one of the earliest NFT collections on Terra to impact personal identification of ecosystem participants.

In summary, this proposal presents a unique opportunity for Terra to: 
- Establish an economic alliance with the Galactic Punk community
- Promoting the Alliance module, its capabilities and innovation
- Increasing on-chain activity.
- Provide identity to all ecosystem participants, builders and community members.


With this partnership, LUNA stakers and the Terra community as a whole stand to benefit from increased exposure and engagement.

We look forward to, and encourage your feedback.
