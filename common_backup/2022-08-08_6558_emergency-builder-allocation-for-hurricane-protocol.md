## **Overview**

Hurricane Protocol is a fully decentralized protocol offering private transactions on Terra 2.0, with Yield Farming and NFT trading. The main motivation behind the strategy is to provide the maximum security to the Terra Network, which is foremost displayed in transfers amongst users.
Privacy technology is expanding across blockchains. The creation of zk-Snarks, a "zero-knowledge proof" in the cryptography world enables completely private transactions.
So the Hurricane team implemented zk-Snarks, "zero-knowledge proof" on our Hurricane privacy application on Terra 2.0.
Hurricane Protocol will also provide Dex tools, Vault and NFT trading for privacy.


## **Goals**

We believe that "Crypto must deliver on privacy!".
Crypto is the only chance we have left for a cash equivalent digital payment option that preserves privacy between sender and receiver. Without this option, society gives up yet another right to privacy.  We now have our platform to make transactional privacy safe and secure.
From now, we are going to expand our platform to DEX Tools, NFT Trading Dapp and Vault to offer every LUNAtic the ability to exercise their right to privacy.

## **Proposed Grant Allocation**

- **Total Distribution**: 90,000 (90k) Luna
- **Vesting Terms**:  Equal to the emergency allocation previously disbursed to the other teams: 50% given after approval, and 50% linear vesting for 3 months;
- **Conditions**: The funds will be managed through a multisig controlled by the emergency council so that it can stop the vesting should the team fail to fulfill the goals that are being set 
- **Use of Funds**: Our team consists of 7 full time employees (Project Manager, Lead Developer, Backend Developer, 2 Frontend Developers, Website Designer & NFT illustrator.). 

             Salaries: Project Manager(2k/week)  Lead Developer (2.5k/week)  Backend Developer(2.5k/week )  
             2 Frontend Developers (2*2k/week) Website Designer (1.75k/week) NFT illustrator (1.75/week)
             Total: 14.5k/week 
             Grant Allocation will be used for employees' salaries for a 3-month period. (13 weeks)
