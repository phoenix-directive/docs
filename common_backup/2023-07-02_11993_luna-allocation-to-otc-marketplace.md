Hello LUNAtics,

Caliber Capital Group is more than just a marketing and consulting agency - we are developing tech to accommodate the Community behind our services that creates an entire ecosystem. For reference, here is our writeup on Terra Money - [https://www.terra.money/ecosystem/caliber-capital-group](https://www.terra.money/ecosystem/caliber-capital-group).

One of these tech implementations being added to said ecosystem is our OTC Marketplace (OTCM). Our OTCM will be utilized to allow our NFT holders to purchase Caliber's investments. When Caliber invests into a protocol or client, we buy up a portion of Team allocated tokens. In doing so, we provide Teams the ability to pay their staff and increase runway without having to remove liquidity behind their token to do so. As most OTC deals are performed at a discount comparatively to market price, we are then able to share that discount with our Caliber Conduit (utility pass) NFT holders that have access to our OTCM. We provide these discounts to our Community taking into account time as well as a monetary value. For example:

Caliber purchases 1000 tokens from a Team allocation, trading at $1, for 900 USDC. We can then sell a portion of these tokens on our OTCM for a 5% discount comparative to the current market price (when token price drops below a certain threshold, purchases will be paused). To avoid mass dumping from this immense sell pressure now created, we create a lockup period from 30-180 days that a user can choose. The longer the lockup, the more tokens received from our algorithm. A user that buys 100 USDC worth of the token with a 30 day lock up period will not receive as many tokens as someone who buys 100 USDC with a 180 day lockup. Caliber is then able to utilize the USDC to replenish our treasury spend from this particular investment. In full, Teams/Clients are now liquid with runway without having to pull liquidity from their token, Caliber is able to turn a small profit for a portion of their investment in the short term with shortned lock-up periods, as well as providing opportunity to our Community comprised of the "average retail investor" that wouldn't normally have access to these types of opportunities.

What I am proposing is an allocation of $LUNA to Caliber's OTCM, to then be repaid in USDC that will allow future funding potential from the Terra Community in USDC instead of LUNA. There will be times when stables are needed for payments. Having USDC available for allocation allows for Teams to avoid selling $LUNA to meet a USDC payment for whatever pursuit of their protocol. Simply put, I think it can't hurt to have an extra funding option for proposals. We would like to propose a smaller number, 6500 LUNA, to allow the Terra Community to see how the OTCM operates before a potentially larger allocation. Caliber will not be taking a 5% profit as mentioned above, but rather a 2% service fee **ONLY IF APPROVED FOR A SECOND LARGER ALLOCATION - this particular 6500 will be completely repaid in USDC**. The ability to purchase this LUNA allocation will not be gated behind our NFT. We can even develop a proxy contract to attach to our OTCM's smart contract that sends the USDC directly to TFL or the funding source of this allocation if need be.

Happy to provide clarity to any information listed above and welcome any critiques or suggestions. Thank you for your consideration.

-Mr Mohawk
President
Caliber Capital Group
