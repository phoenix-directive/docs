Galactic Mining Club: Hash-Powered NFTs on the Blockchain
Grant Proposal
September 2023


Executive Summary
2 / 4
Galactic Mining Club aims to provide real utility in the Non-Fungible Token (NFT) space by utilizing our Bitcoin mining facility to generate real revenues for NFT holders within the Cosmos ecosystem.  In addition to Bitcoin, the club may mine other profitable cryptocurrencies to provide a diversified portfolio to holders, while introducing further liquidity into the Cosmos ecosystem.  The Galactic Mining Club will utilize fees generated by the Club to create additional revenue streams utilizing various Decentralized Finance (DeFi) strategies. All revenues are shared amongst NFT holders or reinvested into the Club.  The Galactic Mining Club has been operational for two years, having partnerships with Codex (formerly known as WeFund), and multiple mining pools to bring additional incentives to holders. Due to the Terra 1.0 collapse in 2022, Galactic Mining Club took a hiatus to re-evaluate market conditions and re-develop an operational plan moving forward.
Team
The team behind Galactic Mining Club are a diverse collection of individuals from across the globe, from diverse backgrounds. We volunteered to join the Club to support its mission and goals. A full reveal of team members is planned in the coming weeks.
Purpose
The NFT space has been maturing since its inception only a few years ago. No longer satisfied with mere images, many NFT projects are promising long roadmaps with coming utility to holders to provide a reason to continue holding the project. A stark decline in the value and market capitalization of even the most prominent NFT projects has occurred in recent years as a result. Some of these projects saw as much as 80-90% of their value evaporate, with some reports that up to 95% of NFTs have no inherent value. Galactic Mining Club aims to upend this trend by providing an ongoing incentive to continue holding by sharing profits generated through cryptocurrency mining (particularly Bitcoin). This, in essence, makes NFT holders a shareholder in future profits generated by the Club, and also provides an introduction and exposure to mining for those who may be interested in mining but lack the funds or technical prowess to set up their own mining operations.
As part of building a viable, self-sufficient revenue stream for the project to benefit NFT holders, it is identified that this may need to be subsidized during the early stages of the project.  The team, as a result, is seeking $100,000 USD equivalent in Luna.  If necessary, a portion (<20%) may be utilized to fund additional mining equipment during the initial phase, however, the majority of these funds will be staked across an array of validators. The staked Luna will provide a base of revenue for token holders to incentivize purchase & holding, while a portion of the staking rewards will be directed to the Club’s Treasury to initiate DeFi strategies. This will also create additional revenue for the various validators that are selected. This staked Luna, we propose, will be returned to the Community Pool within three years once the Club has become self-sufficient and managed by a multi-sig wallet.

Objective/Outcomes
3 / 4
Galactic Mining Club’s roadmap provides both short-term and long-term goals for the project. This includes:  
1. The production of a limited-run NFT collection. The founder of Omniflix is personally creating the artwork for this collection – Target Date: January 2024
2. Securing an NFT staking platform for disbursement of revenue. It is important to note that there is ongoing discussions about the potential to inscribe a Lightning wallet into the NFT’s code to allow the NFTs to receive Bitcoin directly. However, alternatives are also being explored to disburse native assets earned through mining, such as Maya Protocol, Nomic BTC, or similar – Target Date: March 2024
3. The use of a governance model to allow NFT holders the ability to vote in the future direction of the Club – Target Date: April 2024
4. Development of DeFi strategies, including staking and other delta-neutral or low risk investment strategies. This may include the use of various DeFi platforms, depending on funds available and available strategies – Target Date: May 2024
5. Expand available Bitcoin miners to increase yield, while researching and adding other cryptocurrencies that can be obtained through Proof of Work consensus – Target Date: July 2024
6. Long term expansion of the hardware facility, adding additional equipment, and additional facilities, where possible.  Future sites will consider a variety of factors, including power costs, power sources (e.g. renewable versus nonrenewable), and local cryptocurrency regulations Target Date: 2025
Plan
As outlined previously, much of what Galactic Mining Club requires is already in development, or available through various protocols within the Cosmos ecosystem. Currently, Galactic Mining Club has a 2-storey, 3200 square foot facility located in upstate New York. This facility is ready to go, with power and ventilation in place, and operational hardware already. Given the current market conditions, urgency will be afforded to securing additional mining equipment where costs may be more beneficial than during stronger markets.  
Upon the completion of the NFT collection, a mint date and price will be set. At this time, an organic, guerilla marketing campaign is planned as many members of the Galactic Mining Club are already imbedded in various Cosmos ecosystems, including Atom, Stargaze, Terra, and Kujira. There will be various social channels, including Discord and Telegram, to connect with community members and grow the Club.  All revenues generated from the mint will go to the Club’s Treasury to be staked or utilized in the future (this may include diversification of these assets to ensure a robust Treasury). Ongoing royalties will also be handled in this manner. Initial DeFi strategies may include the use of DeFi platforms to loan funds for a reliable, low risk return.
Once the NFT roll out is complete, the ability to stake will launch, opening up further opportunities to engage the community and allow for governance to contribute to the future of the project. It is important to note that NFTs that are not staked (e.g. those for sale on the market,
4 / 4
or held in a wallet) will not be entitled to disbursement of mining revenue. This incentivizes the use of the staking platform, creating buy-side pressure due to diminishing availability.
While every effort will be made to ensure Galactic Mining Club is as decentralized as possible, given the nature of many components to operations, including hardware operations, cross chain swaps, and revenue disbursement, it is recognized that a certain element of centralization is inevitable. Team members will ensure they Abstain from votes that would involve their roles/responsibilities in some way.
Evaluation
As part of the evaluation of the success of the Galactic Mining Club, an ongoing evaluation will occur of both the NFT collection, and revenue generated. This may include:
1. Adjustment to NFT collection size if it fails to mint out, or as a way to increase the revenue generated by each NFT. This may be a temporary reduction (e.g. team collecting and holding NFTs that are unstaked, and therefore, not generating revenue), or a permanent reduction (burn) depending on the market conditions at the time.

2. An ongoing, monthly evaluation of the sustainability and viability of the revenue stream. This includes adequate yield and uptime from the hardware, generating revenues to cover all necessary expenses, and providing a positive return to the community.
The goal of the evaluation is to ensure the long-term sustainability and viability of the Galactic Mining Club. It is presumed that the revenues generated through mining endeavors and DeFi strategies will allow Galactic Mining Club to become self-sufficient within 3 years.
