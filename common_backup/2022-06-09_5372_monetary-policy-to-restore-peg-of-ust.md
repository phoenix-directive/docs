**Monetary Policy to Restore Peg of UST**
Hi Everyone.
For currency to maintain or restore value, trust is imperative.

Company advisors and decision makers must recognise that the value of the UST stablecoin has and will always be a real reflection of Terra as a whole.

**_UST is Terra's medium of exchange._ **
Any attempts to replace or abolish this token will inevitably end in disastrous outcomes for all parties involved.

**Advice to Policymakers: Focus on Monetary Policy Implementation and Liquidity Function Elasticity.**

The value of UST can be restored by implementing monetary policies and exercising control over liquidity preference functions.

1) Use monetary policy to create **aggregate shocks** in the supply of UST in circulation.

2) Collaborate with your liquidity providers to limit the flow of UST, making the **money inelastic** in the short run.

If properly implemented,
- [ ] Aggregate supply shocks will create upward impulses in the demand for UST 
- [ ] Controlling liquidity functions will alter market perceptions of scarcity in the supply of UST
- [ ] _**Wider Perceived Scarcity  = Higher Perceived Value**_



To avoid misunderstanding, I attempted to summarise my recommendation.

If more information is required, I am happy to provide it.
