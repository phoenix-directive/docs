Phoenix Directive has been working on a proposal to merge the current chain alliances into the Liquidity Alliance. With the upcoming shutdown of Alliance Hub by TFL ([https://x.com/terra_money/status/1838821914397315501](https://x.com/terra_money/status/1838821914397315501)), it is now time to move forward with the proposal.



**Proposal**



This proposal builds on the success of the Terra Liquidity Alliance. It will reform the existing Alliance incentive programs to strengthen the chain’s current priority for Terra-focused growth and development. It provides equal or better opportunities for existing projects and chains that benefit from current Alliance programs, enabling them to participate in a fair, competitive structure within the Liquidity Alliance. It also opens the way for new Alliance partners and their blue chip tokens to come to Terra, while ensuring that their participation contributes to growing a war chest of cross chain assets within Terra’s community pool.



The new system will provide greater opportunities for projects and liquidity providers, as well as foster innovation, while strengthening Terra’s position as a leading platform for DeFi and Liquidity.



Key Adjustments:







* **New Liquidity Alliance**: Introduce a new incentive structure targeting a single asset and external LST LP category. (proposed rate of 5%) 

* **Chain Alliance Rewards**: Set to 0%, with immediate unstaking options for current participants (no unbonding period). (current rate 3%)

* **ampROAR Alliance Rewards**: Set to 0%, with a standard 21-day unstaking period for current participants. (current rate 0.4%)





### **Rationale for Proposal**



With the winding down of Terraform Labs (TFL) and the decommissioning of the Alliance Hub, it is the right time for Terra to shift its focus to more efficient and aligned incentive systems. Phoenix Directive will continue basic maintenance of the Chain Alliance, but continuing its current structure does not benefit Terra's economy for the following reasons:







1. **Disparity in Incentives**: Terra spends approximately $1,000,000 annually on incentives for external chains, yet receives only $7,200 in return.

2. **Lack of Additional Integrations**: The Chain Alliance initiative has not led to significant new integrations. The current incentive mechanism has proven ineffective in driving further adoption.

3. **Independent Adoption of Alliance Module**: Chains like Pryzm and Persistence have integrated the Alliance module without needing incentives, suggesting that motivated chains will continue to adopt the module for its core benefits.

4. **Prioritising the growth of Terra economy:** Through the innovative use of the Alliance module Terra will provide the strongest use case for Alliance adoption on other chains. Over time this network will enable the formation of value creating cross chain Alliances.

5. **Resource Reallocation**: Sunsetting the Chain Alliance frees up resources for Terra’s Liquidity Alliance, which has already shown compelling results in restoring liquidity and boosting community engagement.

6. **Avoiding Double Spending**: Maintaining the Chain Alliance alongside the new Liquidity Alliance would result in duplicating incentives, undermining the new system’s competitiveness.

7. **Cost Savings**: The costs incurred in maintaining the Chain Alliance, can be more effectively used to support the development of new growth initiatives on Terra, e.g. required tools like [https://dashboard.alliance.money/](https://dashboard.alliance.money/) are not working anymore.



More importantly, Terra Liquidity Alliance is now live providing a superior solution for allocating rewards and developing Terra. The initial launch has seen significant success in bringing Liquidity back to Terra, making the community excited about Terra and bringing the most advanced Liquidity Provisioning and Acquisition system to Terra and Cosmos. [https://commonwealth.im/terra/discussion/24385-launching-terra-liquidity-alliance](https://commonwealth.im/terra/discussion/24385-launching-terra-liquidity-alliance)



Furthermore Liquidity Alliance will provide a more uniform and fair way to allocate rewards for projects, while also allowing for healthy competition, supporting project liquidity and single asset economies and dApps.



**Single Asset Liquidity Alliance**: Propose an alliance with a share of 4% staking rewards for single asset and LST liquidity
