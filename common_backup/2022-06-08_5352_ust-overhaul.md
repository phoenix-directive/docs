**Summary**

Anchor should apply DAO based central banking buying and selling operation for UST pegging against USD. 

**Motivation**

To solve the problem of UST depeging trouble against USD especially in the bear trend of crypto asset market, the algorismic pegging model on UST and emergency reserve management for liquidity on LFG were not effective and scalable approach as we had seen on LUNA Crush. 

Thus, we must establish a new approach. Anchor Protocol play a critical role here. 

**Proposal**

First of all, token economics between UST and LUNA is very scalable model. Thus, we don’t have to change this. 

 Instead, we don’t need to maintain around 20% of UST staking as always on Anchor. Because, in bull trend, crypto investors prefer to chose invest in other risk asset or DeFi. Thus, it’s very natural that lower demand for UST staking even with 20% APY. Thus, Anchor should set lower interest rate for UST staking.

 Then, Anchor should store the reserve with the above lower interest rate during crypto bull trend.

On the other hand, when crypto market turns to be bearish, it’s great chance for UST to scale their circulation supply. Thus, Anchor should hike their UST staking interest rate. But, here, Anchor should use the above treasury to maintain its UST peg against USD to avoid UST depeg trouble.

In parallel, since larger circulation supply requires larger treasury reserve, LUNA should set and manage issuance ceiling based on DAO voting. 

Here is an example to help you understand for the above proposal.

Let’s suppose that we have $100M worth of bLUNA as collateral on Anchor. 

LTV is 200%. Thus, UST lending size will be $50M as a maximum. 

DPoS rewards on LUNA is 12% APR. Thus, for $50M UST lending, Anchor can make 24% APY. 

Thus, the total rewards which Anchor can make is $12M worth of bLUNA.

Then, in the case of that crypto asset market is in bull trend, UST staking demands will be lower. Thus, let’s say Anchor set UST staking rewards at 5%. Here is 19% reward gap between 5% and 24%. Thus, Anchor can reserve $9.5M worth bLUNA staking rewards. This rewards should go to the treasury reserve for maintaining UST peg.

Also, Anchor must hold this rewards as USDC or USDT because Anchor use these reserve for buying operation on UST in bear trend just as central banks do. BTC is not accurate treasury reserve for maintaining UST peg because of its volatility. 

Then, the based on the amount of the above reserve size, LUNA should decide UST circulation supply based on DAO voting. Let’s say the treasury reserve should keep 10% of the total supply, etc. We need more accurate simulation model for this supply management. 

For the last part, here is 1st principle thinking for the stability for decentralized stable coin. 

Why USD is stable?

Simply because USD has been developing diversified liquidity such as housing, food, shopping, finance, and work. Such large amount of actual transaction demands minimize speculative trading impact on its value. 

 Thus, along with the above solution, LUNA2.0 should focus on diversifying UST liquidity in long term. The below will be the minimum requirement. 

1.UST Pools on Curve
2.UST Pairs on Astroport
3.UST on NFT Marketplaces
4.UST on NFT Games.
