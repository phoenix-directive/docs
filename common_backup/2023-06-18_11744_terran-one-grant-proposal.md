Dear Friends,

TERRAN.ONE is requesting 85,000 LUNA (~$48k USD at time of writing) from the community pool. We are currently in the process of raising capital and these funds, if approved, would support us with enough liquidity during this period to continue development on several important developer tooling projects that are underway.

We have been able to stretch out the funds we received in our Emergency Grant Allocation for about a year thanks to our validator, and have refrained from asking the community for funds unless absolutely necessary.

**About Us**
TERRAN.ONE is a R&D lab focused on projects that aim to improve developer experience on Terra, originally self-funded and bootstrapped. We've released several open source developer tools such as:

- cosmwasm-vm-js: a CosmWasm runtime that can be executed in Node.js, React Native, and other JavaScript environments
- CWSimulate: a fully-featured framework and library for visualizing, debugging, and testing smart contracts with support for multiple blockchains
- cosmwasm-as: a proof-of-concept framework for writing CosmWasm contracts in AssemblyScript (subset of TypeScript)


You can find our projects on our GitHub:

[https://github.com/terran-one](https://github.com/terran-one)

**What we're working on**
Our current project is CWScript, a simplified language for writing smart contracts on Terra. This language was designed, developed, and tested against Terra and aims to improve accessibility for new Terra projects. We have had to temporarily halt development due to funding, but we're about 5 weeks out from public release.

You can learn more about CWScript on our documentation site (work in progress):
[https://cwscript-documentation.vercel.app/](https://cwscript-documentation.vercel.app)

**Planned Spending**
We primarily intend for this grant to provide additional liquidity to support the cash flow generated by our validator. According to conservative projections, this would allow us to continue development for around **4 months**. 

**Expenses**
- Tooling Engineer 1 - $4000/mo
- Tooling Engineer 2 - $3500/mo
- Tooling Engineer 3 - $3500/mo
- DevOps - $3000/mo
- Infra - $4500/mo (Terra-specific subset)

If you have benefitted from our tooling or sympathize with our mission of building for builders, please consider supporting us during these time.

In solidarity,
