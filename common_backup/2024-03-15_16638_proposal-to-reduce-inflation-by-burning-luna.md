_**Disclaimer:**_ I am an avid supporter of $Luna.

**Context:**
Currently, we can see that $Luna is being excessively supplied to the market. The total supply of $Luna is 1,164,290,658 according to https://www.mintscan.io/terra/assets

On a daily basis, around 200,000 $Luna is being supplied to the market (over 70 million annually - inflation: 7%). The supply is significantly higher compared to current demand.

Therefore, I propose the following solution:

Burning $Luna through fees

**1. Burning through TFL-developed tools**
As the community has funded TFL with 125M $Luna (As per the proposal here: https://commonwealth.im/terra/discussion/13682-tfl-community-grant), TFL will not use fees from its tools for the company's operating expenses.

**a. Station**

For the Terra chain:
Currently, Station has the highest user base on the chain and generates significant fees. We know that these fees will be rewarded to stakers. Now, these fees will be burned.

Example:
According to calculations: March 15, 2024
then

	- Theoretical: ~13.868% [inflation based only]

	- Practical: ~16.504% [measured in the last 24h]

So, Station is currently generating about 3% in fees. Burning 3% of this will reduce inflation to 4%.

For chains other than Terra:

20% of the fees generated by Station will be used. For example, Chain A, if Station fees are 10,000 Coin A/1 day. 20% x 10,000 Coin A will be used to swap to $Luna for burning. The remaining 80% will follow the old rules.

**b. Other tools if there are fees will be burned, such as Warp...**



**2. For partners: CEX - DEX**
Transaction fees for purchasing $Luna will be increased by 0.05% (Maker / Taker)

_Example:_ 
 	On the Binance Exchange: https://www.binance.com/en/fee/trading

For a regular account, with daily trading of around 20M $Luna - 20M USDT, we can burn 20k $Luna (Maker / Taker) at a price of $1 per $Luna --> In one year, we can burn at least 700,000 $Luna ~ 1% inflation/year.

Thus, we can quickly observe using the Station and Binance tools that inflation can be reduced to 3%. This does not include other tools or other CEX - DEX platforms.


_**Coordination requirements:**_
TFL will establish a treasury for burning for the community to monitor.
TFL will work with CEX to increase transaction fees for $Luna.
