## **Summary**
The proposal aims to incentivize the[ LUNA-USK](https://fin.kujira.app/trade/kujira1zz74gvmq6ss3pg5vgahvx47ugpfzr80qu75l97lf2ggdgxq04ddqxkdzey?q=all) pair on Kujira's[ BOW](https://bow.kujira.app/pools) with 5,000 LUNA for a period of 4-6 weeks. This will be matched with 5,000 KUJI from the Kujira Operational Fund. The desired end result is to further cement the partnership between the Terra ecosystem and Kujira given that a significant number of Kujirans are also Lunatics, to support the growth and adoption of Kujira's products, and to create additional utility (trading, borrowing, lending) for LUNA outside of Terra.
## 
## Proposal
The proposal is designed to incentivize liquidity for LUNA on the BOW market maker, with the goal of deepening liquidity for Terra and increasing the utility of LUNA. The proposed action items for implementing this proposal are as follows:

- Fund a Terra wallet with 5,000 LUNA. This funding could be managed by a trusted community member who would be responsible for using the LUNA to incentivize the LUNA-USK pair on the BOW market maker until the end of January.
- Funding of the Terra wallet should be completed as soon as possible after the proposal is approved.


**Benefits:**

- Cementing a partnership with Kujira and strengthening the bond between our communities helps foster collaboration and cooperation.
- Deepening liquidity for LUNA outside of Terra increases its accessibility and appeal to a wider audience.
- Providing yield farming opportunities for Lunatics gives them an additional way to earn rewards and benefits from holding LUNA.
- A robust orderbook for LUNA on FIN may allow it to be used as collateral, which can open up new possibilities for borrowing and lending activities.


**Risks:**
- Inflationary pressure: Increasing the supply of LUNA could potentially dilute its value. This proposal requests for only 5000 LUNA. Furthermore, this amount will be emitted over the period of 4-6 weeks.
- Counterparty risk: Pairing LUNA with USK on FIN creates counterparty risk for LUNA.
- Risk of campaign failure: There is a risk that the incentivization campaign may not be successful in achieving its objectives.


An example of a community successfully incentivizing liquidity for BOW is the recent example of Secret Network incentivizing SCRT on BOW. The Secret community funded a Secret wallet under temporary custody of a trusted community member, which allowed them to incentivize the[ SCRT-USK](https://bow.kujira.app/pools/kujira10sx8wxzev270zrmpq6z3asgpurdjfh9f6rwtgt55mar9m6gtw40s9nfxcy) pair on BOW. The goal was to incentivize until the end of January, and the community was able to achieve this successfully. Here are some key highlights about BOW:

- BOW is a framework that ensures optimal trading conditions for pairs on FIN by offering yield to liquidity providers who add to the depth of a given trading pair. This narrows the price spread and ensures deep order books for traders, leading to higher volume and stability.
- BOW also enables protocols to further incentivize pool depth by allocating token rewards to liquidity providers.
- BOW is a self-sufficient and 100% on-chain market maker that uses an internal algorithm to automatically place orders on FIN pairs based on its own internal token balances. By holding LP tokens, you can earn a share of the fees from transactions using that pair, and further increase your earnings by staking the LP tokens.


**TL;DR**
This proposal aims to tackle the issue of insufficient liquidity for LUNA on FIN by incentivizing the LUNA-USK pair on the BOW AMM with 5,000 LUNA for a period of 4-6 weeks, which will be matched by the Kujira Operation Fund with 5,000 KUJI. By deepening liquidity and increasing the utility of LUNA, this proposal could potentially support the growth and adoption of the Terra protocol while strengthening relations with the Kujira community.
