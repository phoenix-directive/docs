## **Grant Proposal**

We are asking for US $300,000 worth of LUNA in order to fund 6 months of runway. The grant will go toward sustaining our manpower to keep the lights on as well as to build out our roadmap. 

Expenses are categorized and allocated into 

- Salary (80%). This includes the engineering & product team to ship new features listed above, and the operation team that works on the NFT LaunchPad (ops, DD, marketing, content)
- Infrastructure (20%). This includes operating costs like office rent, gas fees, and fixed software infrastructure costs such as GCP, Pinata, and Cloudinary. 

 
We estimated that this amount of funds would help us maintain our operations and meet roadmap deliverables. 
 
Prior to applying for this grant, we have exhausted alternative options to find ways to survive:

- **Self-generating revenue:** The first responsible option for a company is to sustain the runway by generating cash by itself. While this is the goal that we strive to achieve, the current trading volume is far away from generating a self-sustaining cash flow. 
- **Other blockchain ecosystem grants:** while this can be a viable option for the company to survive in the short term, the funding conditions would certainly distract us from building on Terra. 
- **Fundraising options: **the current volatile market conditions make it extremely challenging to raise venture capital that would enable us to focus on Terra at the moment.
