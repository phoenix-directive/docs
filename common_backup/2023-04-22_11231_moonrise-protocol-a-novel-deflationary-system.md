# Summary:
The MoonRise Protocol is an innovative deflationary system that connects two tokens: Luna, the existing native asset on Terra, and $MOON.

By burning Luna tokens, users can obtain $MOON tokens through a novel, escalating conversion mechanism. Each subsequent Luna token burned yields an additional 0.25% of $MOON tokens compared to the previous transaction.

## Burn Mechanism Details 
All of these are subject for discussion, except for the first 2 bullet points:

- **No minting Luna: **Under no circumstances can $MOON tokens be burned to receive Luna tokens.
- **No Peg:** $MOON has no peg.
- **Conversion Rate: **The initial conversion rate for Luna to $MOON is set at 1-to-0.1.
- **Supply Cap: **The escalating conversion mechanism has no cap, allowing the circulating supply of $MOON to potentially reach trillions of tokens.
- **Conversion Fee:** 1% fee is applied when burning Luna for $MOON, which is distributed among Luna stakers.
- **Conversion Rate Multiplier:** Conversion rate will always yield .25% more (someone strong in modeling may have a more sophisticated view point)



## Reasons to Acquire $MOON
**Transaction Fees:** We propose integrating $MOON into the Terra Network, enabling its use for transaction fees, similar to UST's role in Classic.

**Alliance Asset: **We suggest whitelisting $MOON as an Alliance asset. As a privileged Alliance asset, $MOON would be eligible to receive a designated percentage of staking rewards, enhancing its value and utility within the ecosystem.


## Benefits for Luna Stakers
Assuming a hypothetical 2% reward rate for $MOON as an Alliance asset, it would account for 2% of all staking rewards. With 1 billion Luna at 15% inflation, the 2% reward rate equates to 3 million Luna available annually (1B * 15% * 2%).

If ONLY one person burns a single Luna for $MOON, they could obtain all 3 million Luna. Consequently, the escalating conversion mechanism would encourage continuous Luna burning for $MOON to secure a share of the staking rewards. 

The innovative feature of the escalating conversion mechanism, where_ each burned Luna token yields an increasingly larger amount of $MOON, creates a persistent incentive for users to burn Luna in order to secure a share of the 3 million staking rewards available._ This helps expand Luna's use case to a store of value.

In summary, $MOON's unique selling points (USP) are its use for transaction fees and as an Alliance asset. Luna's USP would expand to encompass its role as a store of value.
