# Problem Exposition
Since the Black Swan event of March 2022, Terra and Her ecosystem have struggled to remain relevant to the broader decentralized economy. The launch of a new, independent successor chain _phoenix-1_ was supposed to mark the beginning of the revival of our ecosystem - yet we have witnessed teams exploiting our _Builders Emergency Allocations_ and other _Community Grants_, effectively stealing their allocation and running off to other chains. It was this series of rugpulls that have led the Community to place their faith in the centralized entity TFL, the former core team of the original Terra blockchain, to manage these funding allocations for us while also building new products for the ecosystem back in October 2023.

TFL received a Community Grant for their revival plan in [Proposal 4790](https://dashboard.station.money/proposal/phoenix-1/4790). However, due to their on-going legal scrutiny by the US’ SEC, TFL is unable to fully deliver on some of its own [outlined objectives](https://commonwealth.im/terra/discussion/13682-tfl-community-grant). The focus of this proposal shall lie on:

> TFL to negotiate and pay for community tools/projects using its non-LUNA treasury, ensuring accountability within an enforceable legal framework. We will conduct due diligence on the project’s potential value-add to the ecosystem and its ability to execute. This will relieve a significant burden on the community.

Thursday, 25th April 2024, TFL announced they would [withdraw their liquidity](https://twitter.com/terra_money/status/1783519660505522272) in light of their on-going [Chapter 11 bankruptcy filing](https://storage.courtlistener.com/recap/gov.uscourts.deb.191957/gov.uscourts.deb.191957.1.0.pdf) on 21st January 2024. This spurred uncertainty & discourse within the larger Community. An independent observer may come to the conclusion that TFL’s spendings are currently monitored, restricted, and heavily scrutinized as part of this Chapter 11 bankruptcy procedure.

Similarly, the above highlighted objective of Proposal 4790 likely falls into the same category of restricted and/or scrutinized spendings. This is likely the primary reason why TFL has to date not yet established a grant program since the proposal’s approval in October 2023. Such a program is commonly found on other sovereign chains, e.g. [Atom Accelerator](https://www.atomaccelerator.com/), [Osmosis Grants](https://grants.osmosis.zone/), [sei/acc](https://blog.sei.io/sei-acc/), [Neutron Grants](https://www.neutrongrants.com/) & [Neutron Innovators Program](https://www.neutrongrants.com/blog/introducing-the-neutron-innovators-program), and incentivizes teams to build products in our ecosystem. If such a program existed on Terra, I would've already applied to such a grant myself. Over the course of the two years since the Black Swan event, we have not seen a noticeable influx of new developers and teams. Rather, teams have even abandoned Terra in favor of other chains with less legal troubles. Recently, even [Team Ura has announced their departure](https://x.com/uraxyz_/status/1790055903267348589) , adding yet one more to the count.

This proposal is NOT about the fate of the community grant approved in Proposal 4790. In my opinion, TFL has held true on some of their outlined objectives and thus at least deserves some of the grant money. The fate of said community grant would exacerbate the scope of this proposal and should thus be discussed separately.

# Proposal
This proposal seeks to diminish our current degree of reliance on a centralized entity facing such tremendous legal scrutiny. In particular, this proposal seeks to relieve TFL of the above highlighted obligation, and to establish a separate legal entity as non-profit named _Terra Vitalis Project_ in its lieu. Reflective of the contemporary zeitgeist, this entity must not be established within the borders of the USA, as the USA is currently generally considered hostile toward the cryptocurrency industry and privacy as a whole.

## Obligations
The TVP shall concern itself with the following obligations. The overarching objective of the TVP is to attract, train, and foster development in our ecosystem whilst “ensuring accountability within an enforceable legal framework.”

### Developer Relations
As an active member of the Community, I can observe there are significantly more users than builders. Most if not all builders are already running beyond full capacity. As a developer myself and having spoken to and worked with developers in the Cosmos ecosystem, we have a seriously high entry barrier for new developers. This is attributable to two facts: Rust - the primary programming language in which CosmWasm Smart Contracts are currently written - is a notoriously difficult & strict language, and the learning material on CosmWasm itself or the available Cosmos SDK modules is scarce and insufficient.

Developer Relations or _DevRel_ is a field of the IT Industry that describes the “nurturing [of] mutually beneficial relationships between organizations and software developers.” This process includes:
- Producing educational material to attract & train new developers.
- Supporting developers in their needs through:
- Direct technical advice or consulting through A) Networking with other builders from our industry sector, B) Networking with freelancers to provide services such as copy writing, graphic design, web design, audio design, etc., and C) Provisioning of infrastructure & tools.
- Promoting our blockchain, community & ecosystem at industry events such as _AwesomWasm_ or _ETHDenver_.

### Innovation Grants Program
Inspired by [Neutron’s Innovators Program](https://www.neutrongrants.com/blog/introducing-the-neutron-innovators-program), our own _Innovation Grants_ are regularly recurring, salary-like grants designed for individual developers with a drive to experiment & innovate.

Typically, grant programs require a clear product, project plan, and budget projection plan, and the grants are paid out in portions based on defined & accomplished milestones. However, not every developer is also a business person, has a team at their disposal, or is willing to risk the stability of their daily life through self-employment. Thus, the _Innovation Grants_ forego this concept in favor of continued experimentation & innovation. Grant recipients can be thought of as independent employees of TVP in pursuit of projects they think are beneficial to the Community & ecosystem, with continuous support & accountability by TVP.

As part of our Due Diligence procedures, applicants must A) demonstrate a degree of autonomy, B) approach with a concrete idea, C) justify their project, D) provide a proper curriculum vitae, and E) pass KYC procedures. Only then are they considered further for an Innovation Grant. Applicants can apply alone or together, but every applicant must individually enter a relationship with TVP.

For TVP and the Community, it is safer to commit to e.g. $3,500 per month rather than $10,500 (3 months) advance payment. Granular periodic payments limit damages from a malicious actor attempting to defraud TVP or breaching contract by deploying on another chain instead. The lack of up-front payments also prevents value extraction without any delivered work whatsoever through our accountability measures.

### Community Grants Enforcement
Additionally to facilitating _Innovation Grants_, TVP shall assist in Community Grant efforts. Specifically, TVP will assist interested teams in preparing a Community Grant proposal as well as promote discourse around it; and upon successful passing of the proposal, TVP will enter a legally enforceable contractual agreement with the grant beneficiary on behalf of the Community, if the Community so desires.

This procedure has various benefits:
- The decision of which projects should be funded is returned to the Community,
- TVP does not need to accommodate for such grants in its own financial plan, thus keeping our treasury & risk to the Community small, and
- The risk of grant beneficiaries defrauding the Community is minimized through legal enforcement.

I also encourage the Community to explore integration with [gitcoin](https://www.gitcoin.co/) to facilitate quadratic funding events. As part of these events, a grant pool is distributed proportionally to different project proposals by independent third parties. The quadratic funding formula aims to democratize funding of projects by weighing the number of distinct pledgers higher than the total amount pledged, thus shifting the power balance from wealthy individuals to strength in numbers.

## Business Form & Structure
TVP will be formed as a registered non-profit organization with the declared objective to promote and support the development of the Terra blockchain ecosystem. As a non-profit entity, proceeds e.g. from advertising revenue on educational content must be reinvested and can be seen as a relief on community spend, but not a substitute thereof.

In which jurisdiction this entity shall reside is subject to further discussion & research, but generally depends on two factors:
- Favorable taxation laws particularly for non-profit organizations, and
- An adequate legal interconnectivity with the various sovereign nations of the world, in order to maximize reach and effectiveness of any legal pursuit if necessary (e.g. extradition treaties).

## Team Composition
To extract the most value of our limited funds, team members will likely decorate multiple roles, much like a startup. Every team member will participate in grant interviews and in supporting the grant beneficiaries. Every team member will be an independent contractor in service of the TVP. Most team members will most likely be employed only part-time. Roughly, the team will consist of:
- **CEO.** Handles bureaucratic madness such as registering the legal entity, and is responsible for measuring the program’s effectiveness over time by KPIs. For the foreseeable future, will also be responsible for accounting & bookkeeping.
- **DevRel x2.** These will produce educational content for developers explaining the CosmWasm stack & demonstrating how to build Dapps, as well as supporting the beneficiaries on a technical level. I am bringing in a DevRel friend & have support from another eager technical community member.
- **Community Managers x2.** In this context, essentially non-technical DevRel. These will keep beneficiaries accountable and help them network & promote their work in the broader community.
- **Elected Council.** The council is the final governing body to approve or reject grant applications and oversee TVP spendings in general, and consists of 4 elected Community members + the TVP CEO. This delegation is intended to grant the Community direct influence into the approved projects, as well as avoiding introducing TVP as a single point of failure for the ecosystem’s success.
- **External freelancers**, e.g. for educational material produced directly by the TVP, or materials needed by the grant beneficiaries.

Future roles to be filled as the project grows include:
- **Accountant.** Accounting & bookkeeping wrt. grants & organization spendings, as well as financial strategies (e.g. to convert parts of the funds into stablecoins for payments via AMM or OTC).
- **BizDev.** Our network simply cannot be large enough.

The exact decoration of these roles is not yet finalized. Specifically, Accountant & BizDev are yet missing, and will be filled later.

## Multi-Level DAO
TVP will secure its funds through a multi-level DAO setup:
- **TVP Treasury** holds the bulk of the funds. It consists of exactly two members: TVP Operations and TVP Council. Requires a 100% quorum and threshold, without fast-tracking.
- **TVP Operations** is the operational multi-signature DAO filled by TVP management, and will hold operational assets such as short-term salaries and material budgets. It receives funds from the Treasury upon mutual approval. Requires 50% quorum & 51% threshold, with fast-tracking.
- **TVP Council** is composed of the aforementioned elected council. Requires a 50% quorum & 51% threshold, without fast-tracking.

## KPIs
The efficacy and success of TVP can indirectly be measured by the following key performance indicators:
- **Luna price.** Naturally, if the TVP is successful, the developments on the Terra blockchain supported by the TVP should contribute to a thriving ecosystem and be implicitly reflected in a positive trend of its native token Luna.
- **Active Wallets.** A vibrant ecosystem of applications should provide users with diverse opportunities to engage, invest, and trade. This growth should be implicitly reflected in a positive trend of the daily & monthly active wallets.
- **Total Value Locked.** A vibrant ecosystem of applications should attract more and more exogenous capital. However, simply measuring the TVL across the various smart contracts of Terra is, historically, a deceptive metric, particularly when derivative protocols begin double-counting the same capital.
- **Number of Active Teams**, including solo devs under the framework of the TVP. Naturally, as the declared objective of the TVP non-profit is the promotion of the development of the Terra ecosystem, we should keep track of the growth of the development landscape.
- **Grant Accounting.** In order to achieve and maintain maximum efficiency of the allocated capital, TVP must periodically prune underperforming beneficiaries. Performance shall be loosely based on grant spending versus profitability of each beneficiary’s products, provided they set out to build end user facing products. Beneficiaries who build infrastructure, tooling, and other development related projects will be measured by their impact on the broader Cosmos ecosystem.

# Use of Funds
_Innovation Grants_ shall be paid out on a salary basis in **USDC and/or Luna equivalent** to beneficiaries on the 1st of every month. Beneficiaries shall receive up to $6,000 monthly depending on their time commitment and seniority, the latter naturally to be determined upon conduct of due diligence. Beneficiaries may choose the ratio of their salary in USD & Luna. Bonuses in Luna shall be awarded upon achievement of certain individual milestones relating to each beneficiary’s projects, such as 500 unique recurring Warp jobs created by their project. Further alignment incentives shall be determined throughout the project, such as fee share module royalties or matching [tea.xyz](https://tea.xyz/) revenue.

With above team composition, we shall assume an annual budget of ~$180,000 for salaries or ~$15,000 per month. To begin with, we reserve a monthly Alliance target of ~$30,000, to be adjusted as the number of grant beneficiaries varies, but chosen to minimize the number of required governance proposals. Excess Luna shall be returned to the community pool at the start of each new month. Funds in excess of the actual need are used to build a stablecoin reserve to provide stability to the project even during economic uncertainty. TVP will prefer OTC deals e.g. on [Fuzion Plasma](https://plasma.fuzion.app/) and/or other OTC platforms to avoid supply shocks on the open market.

# Source of Funds
The funding of this program shall consist of donations from the Community and the Community Pool. To kickstart the efforts, this proposal seeks an initial funding of $250,000 worth in LUNA, to be used to compensate team members for their work as well as one-time & upfront expenses such as lawyer & creditor fees for the legal entity and its contracts, or the promotion at Web 3 conferences.

A follow-up proposal shall seek to register a new virtual token as Alliance Asset with a weight of 0.009, or ~0.85% of the current inflation of 7%, including current Alliances. At a token price of ~$0.60, this provides TVP with ~$425,000 per year, or ~$35,000 per month, as well as leeway for fluctuations in token price.

To warrant time for the program to show effectiveness, I recommend the program be reevaluated in January 2025. Beneficiaries will be required to demonstrate the ability to self-organize and rapidly deliver & iterate within a quarter to half a year, lest risk termination of their grant. All projects developed under the grant shall be beneficial to the Terra ecosystem, the stakers & Community Pool, and fully open source. The grants will prioritize DeFi protocols, although infrastructure and developer tooling may also be considered.

# Conclusion
Without TFL, we likely wouldn't be where we are today. However, the situation even today is rather dire. Terra & Her Community should diversify in the entities we rely on, as evidently a single point of failure is now threatening our survival.

Thank you for reading. Discourse & suggestions are, of course, welcome.

~Kiru
