## Summary
[Confio](https://confio.gmbh/) is one of the core development teams in the cosmos ecosystem. They build and maintain the smart contract subsection (among other things). This proposal is help them continue this by funding them with ~60,000USD/month. (which is ~20% of their total monthly spend).

## Motivation
The Terra chain is built upon solid foundations. We need to ensure these foundations are well maintained, and have groups of people who are motivated to enhance and maintain the various components we rely on.

One of these foundational companies is Confio. They are responsible for the cosmwasm smart contract layer, as well as the WASM sandbox the smart contracts run in (among other things).

Not only will this provide them with funds to pay their developers, it will give our core development team access to priority support from them.


## Proposal
We are proposing the creation of an mechanism that will enable us to pay them the equivalent of 60k  USD/month, which represents 20% of their monthly costs. 

We can do this by depositing ~750k USD worth of tokens into a enterprise DAO and releasing a months worth of funds at a time. (and then resubmitting this proposal again in 12 months time)

_or_ we could do an endowment where the yield is ~60k/month and is self-sustaining. An endowment would provide a reliable source of funding, allowing them to continue their work without the constraints of limited resources.

These funds would be converted to fiat by the Confio team, and would go to paying salaries and other costs around running their business.

## About the team
[Confio](https://confio.gmbh/#team) is made up of 20-30 individuals, and is led by Ethan Frey, Martin Worner, & Simon Warta, and have been contributing to the cosmos ecosystem for 2-3 years.


## Next Steps
I hope to discuss how we can fund this. and if we need a multi-sig/enterprise DAO who should be on there.

## Disclaimer
I am not an employee of financially linked to Confio.

It was a nice discussion on Twitter Spaces yesterday and I promised to follow up giving more details on what we can offer. Thank you PFC for quick follow up post, and I would like to expand on it. This is basically correct:

- New features from them, including them listening to our needs 
- Advice & Guidance to our devs
- Emergency support
- Better documentation


First off, we invite all terra devs to join [https://chat.cosmwasm.com](https://chat.cosmwasm.com) where there is some ecosystem-wide support. Especially on ibc contracts it brings together the half-dozen or so teams really building that out, and you can get feedback from what others are building. Confio employees are also active there and answer some questions (usually harder ones that go unanswered by the community). Pro tip: Introducing yourself (and what project you are working on, maybe even github link) can get faster answers and differentiate you from n00bs. This invitation is independent of any funding, but funding does buy more attention.

Secondly, we are happy to have discussions with a few contact people from the Terra community as to what Terra needs. And either explain how that can be done with the current stack, and/or add missing features to our backlog. Since it is very hard to filter a larger group of people who all want something (and might just misuse "Terra" label to get attention), we would like to have 2-3 people to talk to, that can bring a list of top issues to us (and we can follow up directly with the teams that need it if we need more info, etc) It sounds like this might be [@Jared](https://agora.terra.money/profile/id/45001), [@Somethingelse](https://agora.terra.money/profile/id/56408) & @MC

We have made changes before for Terra (as we got some grants from TFL), such as adding queries and custommsg in 2020, to later improving our ability to handle parallel queries and hunting down small memory leaks that only showed up during Terra's massive server load. We need to know what you all think it important. We gave a rough list of what features we hope to add this year in the hub proposal (read page 4-5 [https://gateway.pinata.cloud/ipfs/QmTYBVYadb4WmhZjq5Vo7hSmFXdxBdcPowVa6nnh1HCMPu](https://gateway.pinata.cloud/ipfs/QmTYBVYadb4WmhZjq5Vo7hSmFXdxBdcPowVa6nnh1HCMPu)). Big ones are sdk 0.47 support, and **allowing contracts to use floats**. We need to know what matters to you.

Currently the two items we heard from Terra to add to our roadmap:

- Better documentation - If this proposal passes, we will hire a full time maintainer for the documentation to bring it together, and work with internal and external devs to ensure this is an easy onboarding process. Some of this is just directing people to existing resources, others filling in the gaps, and furthermore expanding code-level docs on complex topics (like IndexedMap).
- Support on IBC - Alliance is being built with IBC and may need features, or maybe just advice. We are happy to listen to their needs and see how to extend the IBC support story as needed. We have recently pushed some enhancements to IBC in 0.31 and few more small additions are coming in 0.32, to handle some advanced use cases that came up. We'd like to hear your story and see what is needed there.


Finally, this is a way to "align incentives" and bring Terra high up on our radar. This means if you hit serious issues in production, we will do our best to help you out (such as chain halt, or some bug on major dapp). Or lesser but annoying things like rpc nodes constantly crashing. This should be considered emergency support, not all the time, but we are definitely happy to work with friendly chains to recover as quickly as possible from any issue.
