**PURPOSE**

This proposal is being put forward as a result of discussions and feedback received from members of the Terra community.

A YES vote on this proposal will suspend funding to Confio until further notice and return remaining allocated funds back to Terra’s Community Pool.


**SUMMARY**

The community pool currently makes monthly payments of 46,000 LUNA to Confio. Two payments have been made to date, totalling 92,000 LUNA.

This signalling proposal if passed would suspend the monthly payments to Confio passed by governance proposal #4723 until further notice.

It will save Terra community pool 461,900 LUNA in payments currently earmarked for Confio that remains in the Confio Enterprise DAO.

Remaining funds in the Confio community grant multisig II Enterprise Dao established to facilitate these payments would be returned to the community pool after the passing of this proposal.


**RATIONAL**

_**Alignment**_ 
While Terra is rolling out Alliance, Confio devs are helping build Mesh Security. We are essentially funding an entity building a product going after the exact same target market as Alliance.

**_Acknowledgement_ **
Confio has not acknowledged Terra’s funding contribution in a meaningful way. After 3 months,Terra has still not been added to the list of sponsors on the Cosmwasm website despite requests.

_**Support**_ 
Since funding has started, neither Terra’s core development team at TFL nor any significant group of builders on Terra have expressed a need for specific assistance from Confio on Cosmwasm or other code related matters.  

**_Spending_ **
This is not the time for Terra to be giving away community pool funds to support public goods projects without immediate benefit to Terra’s own ecosystem growth. Funding of public goods projects should be considered when Terra is in a stronger financial position to justify them.

_**Reporting**_  
Terra has not received a report outlining the value that Terra’s contribution has made including any direct benefits to Terra. Their Medium articles describe general work on Cosmwasm upgrades only.

_**Accessibility**_ 
Responsiveness to information requests from Confio is limited. Their only window to the community is their Discord and this is poorly staffed with little or no response received from questions. Confio founder Ethan Frey recently announced he is taking a one year sabbatical away from Confio.


This proposal recommends that the community consider reinstating funding to Confio in the future when there is a clearer rationale and more available resources for Terra’s support.
