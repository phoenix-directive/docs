## Summary
As FTX exchange is having several locked Luna 2 from customers, it will be good to have community to devise a method to withdraw Locked Luna 2.0
## Motivation
FTX case is a lesson learnt and there will be other exchanges might be facing same issues as FTX. As a LUNA 2 community , we need to have a way forward to get the Locked LUNA 2 from troubled exchanges. I have contacted FTX customer rep to get their support and there is no adequate response so far.
## Proposal
 1) Removal of locking of LUNA 2 from FTX exchanges to enable a smooth withdrawal
2) 1:1 swap from Locked LUNA 2 to unlocked LUNA 2
