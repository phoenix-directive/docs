Summary
LUNC currently faces a need for a long-term solution to drive price growth, increase market attention, and engage holders in a sustainable way. We propose introducing BoostPad on CoinInn, a mechanism that unites LUNC holders to participate in a low-risk, high-reward opportunity that drives price potential while ensuring no losses.

Motivation
While the LUNC community has focused on burns as a solution, this approach has proven to be unsustainable in the long term. BoostPad offers a sustainable alternative by allowing LUNC holders to actively participate in the growth of LUNC’s value. The goal is to foster a unified approach that can engage holders, create positive market momentum, and ensure the community's effort translates into real growth for LUNC.
Instead of relying solely on burning, BoostPad creates a mechanism where holders are incentivized to work together, enhancing market exposure and creating sustained growth for LUNC.

Proposal
We propose implementing the BoostPad mechanism on CoinInn to establish a 7-day cycle aimed at driving 2X growth for LUNC holders. This program would provide a platform where holders can actively participate in a safe, low-risk solution designed to create a more sustainable path for LUNC’s recovery.
How BoostPad works:
BoostPad is a new model developed by CoinInn to bring more exposure and growth to projects by setting specific goals (e.g., 2X return within 7 days).
LUNC holders can engage by purchasing LUNC tokens through BoostPad.
If the target is reached (e.g., 2X growth), the value of LUNC increases for all participants.
If the target is not met, USDT and LUNC are refunded to participants, ensuring no loss of funds.
Action Items:
Implement BoostPad for LUNC: Set up the 7-day cycle with a 2X growth target for LUNC.
Community Engagement: Activate and engage the community to promote participation and create market activity.
Evaluate and Optimize: After each cycle, review results, optimize the process, and adjust for future cycles to improve the program’s effectiveness.
Timeline:
Week 1: Proposal release and community feedback.
Week 2: Launch of BoostPad for LUNC with initial promotion.
Week 3: Assess the success of the cycle, gather community input, and plan for the next cycle.
