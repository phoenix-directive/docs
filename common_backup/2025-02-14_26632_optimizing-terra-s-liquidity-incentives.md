# Optimizing Terra’s Liquidity Incentives: Aligning Support with Ecosystem Growth

**Purpose and Scope**

This proposal outlines key strategic steps and proposals regarding the Single Bucket in Liquidity Alliance, including decisions about incentives, partnerships, and Terra ecosystem.

**The Role of Single Asset LPs on Liquidity Alliance**

To ensure a thriving and sustainable ecosystem, it is essential to periodically assess the impact of various incentive programs. One of the key components of Liquidity Alliance is the Single Asset LP category, which was designed to support ecosystem projects by enabling project token holders to earn LUNA through liquidity provision. A primary objective of this initiative is to reward builders and contributors who actively develop applications on Terra.

As we continue to refine our approach, we must ensure that Terra's resources are allocated effectively to drive genuine growth and innovation within the ecosystem. This includes evaluating whether current incentive structures align with Terra Phoenix’s overall goals.


---

**Evaluating the Strategic Direction of Carbon and Migaloo**

In the first step we started analyzing single asset alliances with external chains.

In recent discussions with Carbon, it was decided that the Carbon SWTH-rSWTH LP will be phased out from Liquidity Alliance. Instead, LUNA liquidity will be strategically deployed on Nitron, Carbon’s money market. While we would have liked to see Nitron and Demex integrated directly on Terra, their architecture is based upon on-chain modules rather than CosmWasm contracts. This makes direct deployment infeasible. As a result, our collaboration will focus on supporting the Carbon lending market with additional LUNA liquidity, allowing Terra users to engage in leverage activities on a partner chain in a mutually beneficial manner.

Regarding Migaloo, our data indicates that while Terra has been the primary source of revenue for the team over the past three years—providing millions of dollars worth of incentives, supporting their core business, and delegating to their validator—the expected ecosystem contributions have not materialized as anticipated. Despite receiving significant liquidity incentives, trade activity within the ampWHALE-WHALE LP and boneWHALE-WHALE LP has been low. Additionally, marketing and collaboration efforts around these liquidity pools have not yielded the expected engagement, with around 70% of the rewards being allocated to a handful of wallets.

While supporting ecosystem projects is a core objective of Terra, it is reasonable to expect significant contributions that directly enhance the Terra ecosystem. Despite past discussions with White Whale re-aligning its focus toward Terra, recent developments indicate a preference for establishing itself as an ICS Hub member (or migrating to a different chain) rather than deepening its integration within Terra. While this is a valid strategic choice for Migaloo, it is not appropriate for Terra to continue subsidizing this direction.


---

**Proposal & Next Steps**

Given these insights, we propose the following steps to realign incentives with Terra’s growth objectives:



1. **Suspension of Single Asset LP Incentives for ampWHALE-WHALE LP and boneWHALE-WHALE LP** – This measure is intended to encourage White Whale to clarify its strategic direction and assess whether it seeks to remain an integral part of Terra.
2. **Resource Reallocation** – Funds previously allocated to these liquidity pools will be redirected towards other initiatives and protocols that demonstrate tangible contributions to Terra’s ecosystem. In the meantime rewards will be distributed to other single alliance assets.
3. **Content Creator Program** – A new initiative will be introduced, allowing content creators to participate in weekly Terra content creation competitions. The Terra community will be able to reward contributions based on Alliance Voting Power, fostering greater engagement and visibility.
4. **Encouraging White Whale to Submit a Counterproposal** – White Whale is invited to outline a clear roadmap detailing how it intends to expand, develop, and innovate within the Terra ecosystem in a measurable way. If White Whale’s future plans align more with ICS rather than Terra, we respect that decision and wish them success, but continued financial support from Terra should not be expected.
5. **Ongoing Audits of Single Asset LP Projects** – Projects benefiting from Liquidity Alliance incentives are expected to present their 2025 roadmap for Terra to ensure ongoing alignment with ecosystem goals.
6. **Ensuring Effective Fund Utilization** – Terra’s resources are finite, and it is critical to allocate them in ways that maximize growth, attract quality projects, and expand the active user base.

With this proposal we want to ensure that our incentive structures are purpose-driven and prioritize Terra’s growth.

# **Counter-Proposal: Ensuring a Fair and Sustainable Liquidity Incentive Model for Terra’s Ecosystem**


### **Purpose and Scope**
This counter-proposal aims to address concerns raised in the initial proposal regarding Liquidity Alliance’s Single Asset LP incentives and provide a more balanced approach to maintaining Terra’s ecosystem sustainability. While we recognize the importance of allocating incentives strategically, it is essential to ensure that decisions foster long-term collaboration rather than create divisions.


### **Migaloo Foundation’s Intent & Participation**
The Migaloo Foundation (MF), developers of White Whale (WW) and stewards of the Migaloo blockchain, have been active and engaged participants in Terra’s ecosystem over the past years. Recognizing the potential of TLA, MF strategically positioned itself as a committed stakeholder by acquiring and locking LUNA for VP allocation. Over time, MF also began providing liquidity in the LUNA/WHALE as strategic protocol-owned-liquidity (PoL) and utilized the entirety of all rewards accrued to bolster our TLA VP position.
To date, MF holds approximately ~15% of total TLA VP and has actively contributed to the ecosystem by bringing in users, DAOs, and liquidity sourcing protocols, such as Hydro. MF’s ethos is centered around fostering user participation and cross-chain collaboration, and our engagement in TLA reflects this commitment. Additionally, we have allocated incentives, including WHALE liquid staking tokens (LSTs) and DGN (Dungeon Chain), to encourage community voting and deeper TLA participation.
