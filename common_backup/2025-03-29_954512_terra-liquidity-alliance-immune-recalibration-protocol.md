## **Summary**

This proposal introduces a critical recalibration of the Terra Liquidity Alliance (TLA) to protect Terra’s native token (LUNA) from parasitic liquidity structures and ensure that inflation (LUNA rewards) is allocated in a way that strengthens the Terra ecosystem’s immune system rather than draining it.


### Rationale

The TLA currently consists of liquidity pools that fall into two distinct categories:

**1. Symbiotic Pools (LUNA Pairs)**

These pools represent lymphocytes in Terra’s immune system. They:

- Pair LUNA with other strategic assets (e.g., ampLUNA, USDC, ATOM, SOLID, WBTC)
- Increase demand for LUNA through every swap
- Compounding them means re-investing in LUNA
- Allow value to be absorbed to Terra

**2. Commensal and Parasitic Pools (non-LUNA Pairs)**

These pools involve no LUNA but some of them currently compete for inflationary rewards with symbiotic pools — despite contributing less to Terra’s economy.
Examples of both types: USDC–USDT, wETH-wstETH, wBTC–wBTC.axl, ROAR-ampROAR, ATOM-dATOM, ...

These pools:
- Do not generate meaningful volume because the ability to arbitrage is non-existent.
- Compounding them results in net selling of LUNA, in which case they may become parasitic, and begin to act as mercenary extraction channels.
- If not autocompounded (rewarded LUNA is kept or re-invested into other pools), act as probiotics, keeping parasitic pools at bay and even contributing to Terra despite not being symbiotic. 


### **Action Plan**

**1. Relocate all non-LUNA Pools to Single Asset**

The following pools will be reclassified under the Single Asset category, so a maximum of 5% inflation is shared across all non-LUNA pools: This means the following (for both Astroport and White Whale ofcourse):

USDC–USDT

USDC–EURe

USDC–SOLID

This change will:
- Prevent type 2 pools from taking over symbiotic liquidity.
- Introduce passive stablecoin capital into Single Asset, where they may help ward off vampire capital.
- Preserve their utility while removing their ability to weaken LUNA liquidity.


**2. Relocate all LUNA Pools from Single Asset to Blue Chip/Project**

Any LUNA-paired pool currently classified as Single Asset will be relocated to the Blue Chip or Project category. This means the following (Astroport and White Whale):

LUNA-arbLUNA

For this particular pool I suggest Blue chip over Project, because we are moving more pools from the blue chip category than the Project category and there are already 3 LUNA-xxxLUNA pools in the Project category. However, this is open for discussion. The category is not important.

This change will:
- Free up space in the Single Asset section
- Ensure that this lymphocyte's liquidity doesn't get diluted

### Outcome Goals

⚔️ Limit inflation leaks to low-volume, non-LUNA pools, by concentrating in one section, rather than 3

🛡️ Strengthen LUNA’s strategic liquidity and network defense, by increasing Blue Chip/Project liquidity

🔥 Restore Phoenix absorption: we need to make sure inflation is optimized to feed the chain, not bleed it


### Closing

This proposal isn’t about favoritism.

 It’s about turning off the slow internal bleeding and allowing Terra to regenerate in full power, which will increase ALL TVL.

For the Phoenix. For the Future.
