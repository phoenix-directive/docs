**D.E.R.S: **The Terra Poker team has developed a proprietary deck encryption and random shuffling called D.E.R.S (Decentralized Encrypted Random Shuffling). The DERS system allows for complete encrypted dealing and hand distribution. DERS is a prized technological advancement made by the Terra Poker team to ensure that integrity is maintained.

DERS leverages on the ‘Threshold Cryptosystem (TC)” and uses an encrypted card deck to achieve complete secure and secretive dealing. By using the TC operative method, only the player can see their dealt cards, and even the card dealer will not know the cards that have been dealt. Simply put, an encrypted deck is used to deal, and in order to open the hand, the TC method must be employed, keeping the pair completely private.
