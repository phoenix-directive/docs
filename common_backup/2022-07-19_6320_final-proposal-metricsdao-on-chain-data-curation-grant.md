# Overview

With the launch of 2.0, Terra is in a critical phase of maturity and growth. There is an immediate need for more on-chain transparency, effective analytics, and user acquisition.

To that end, this proposal is to deliver **freely available, curated Terra 2.0 data -** enabling it to be queried by builders and analysts for dashboards, insights, and application development. This data will be curated by MetricsDAO and made available in Flipside Crypto’s platform. Flipside Crypto’s platform provides a SQL query editor, data visualization studio, API, and SDK free for all users. 

MetricsDAO will also provide basic analytics that show key on-chain metrics, including daily active addresses, cumulative addresses, daily transactions, blocks produced, etc.

MetricsDAO has the unique ability to curate data and produce analytics for Terra 2.0, which attracts leading Web3 analysts to the ecosystem and drives user growth.
 
## Expected Results
- **Free & Open Data_ -_** transformed and polished on-chain Terra 2.0 data for SQL analytics - as well as visualization and API solutions
- **Critical Network & Protocol Usage Metrics **- a single, simple dashboard to help show Terra 2.0's traction and viability as an ecosystem
- **Educational Content **-** **for analysts of all levels to activate within the Terra ecosystem. Beginners can learn SQL, advanced users can optimize results to deliver sophisticated solutions, including the development of tools for Terra protocols
- **Community Question Sourcing **- Terra contributors and the MetricsDAO community brainstorm analytics requests, which will be turned into bounties 
- **Powerful Marketing Materials **- when analysts participate in the bounty program, they are creating narratives that invite users to new protocols and the network
- **Contributor and Bounty Rewards **- (in LUNA) are delivered into the hands of community members, activating new members and retaining participants.


_**Token retention is also a key value proposition of this proposal - so what is it and why?**_

Token retention is how long an address holds a token once received. It is an indicator of user quality - meaning those addresses who hold for longer periods of time are more likely to be positive contributors to the ecosystem (i.e. active users or builders).

In this vein, MetricsDAO efforts deliver $LUNA directly into the hands of community members helping grow the network through data curation. Token retention of these programs far exceeds comparative liquidity mining (LM) and traditional airdrop mechanisms - where most addresses are in it for the quick buck, and dump tokens immediately upon receiving them.

In past programs, we have distributed_** $100k+**_ to the community.

In similar programs run by Flipside Crypto, NTR (native token recycling) for Terra was **73.4%_._** This means almost ¾ of bounties paid out in $LUNA resulted in staying in the ecosystem.

This NTR number exceeds traditional airdrop retention, which floats around 27%. Our goal is to keep tokens in the ecosystem, distributing them among those who care most.



# The Process

MetricsDAO’s Curation Team will set up a Terra 2.0 data pipeline and develop core data models and abstractions in Snowflake. 

Data models developed by the Curation Team live in public, open source repositories on our [Github](https://github.com/MetricsDAO). We have successfully curated data for both Harmony (ONE) and NEAR, and made the models accessible to analysts via Flipside: [https://flipside.new/](https://flipside.new/). 

The Curation team will also develop simple tooling to showcase the models and the metrics derived as well as documentation for these models. 

Data sourced from data models curated by MetricsDAO are used by Harmony on their blockchain explorer:  [Harmony Blockchain Explorer](https://explorer.harmony.one/).

Outputs from the Curation Team will enable a pilot program with the MetricsDAO system. MetricsDAO attracts top-of-the-line analytical minds and uses a 3-step process to solve analytical challenges for blockchains at scale:

1. Community Brainstorming: To Engage & Educate 
2. Analytics: To Generate Insights & Drive User Onboarding
3. Peer Review: To Create Legitimacy & Promote Your Protocol

# Grant Proposal

We are asking $250,000 worth of LUNA, benchmarked at 12:00 UTC time on Friday, July 29, to fund data ingestion and analytical bounties for the reborn Terra. Benchmarking the LUNA amount to a fixed dollar value is intended to protect both parties from price volatility. Should the governance proposal pass, we will provide a Terra address to the community multi-signers for distribution within 7 days of the vote’s end.

Most of this grant ($230,000) will go toward data curation. This includes payment to data curation contributors, data ingestion, node infrastructure, and warehousing costs. The remainder of the grant ($20,000) will go to a pilot of the 3-Step Analytics Program. The goal of the pilot program is to jumpstart the analytics bounty and user acquisition process for Terra 2.0, the future of which can be evaluated in a distinct proposal.

We appreciate your request for detailed feedback! Your request for details focuses on two elements:
- The costs of enterprise-grade infrastructure and pipeline
- Whether a foundational investment in analytics will benefit Terra 2.0


Regarding the first point, we’re happy to provide our fundamental assumptions. One note: you call out dashboarding and analytics specifically. Compensating community members to build a dashboard that shows Terra 2.0's traction and growth is part of the proposal, but only a minor cost driver. 90% of the cost is allocated towards data curation: establishing and maintaining a data layer that analysts and projects can query easily & leverage to acquire new users and investment. 

The $10k monthly infrastructure cost breaks down as follows:
- $2k/month to run a node, at minimum. This cost estimate comes from Flipside’s internal team, who has experience similar to your own in running a validator. 
- Approx. $1k/month for AWS to supply compute costs and limited storage
- $6,500/month for Snowflake infrastructure. At peak volume, Flipside spent $65k monthly to support the data infrastructure for thousands of Terra analysts and their queries; an average data cost of 10% is a reasonable approximation, given that usage will be minimal at first and will ramp up as Terra 2.0 grows 
- A slender $500/month in personnel costs to maintain and monitor infrastructure & the supporting agreements


As to whether building a “data sportscar” makes sense: curating a blockchain will take months, during which the  ~60 teams that are building on it will continue shipping. MetricsDAO believes in these teams and the future of Terra 2.0. Limited activity now is not representative of the thriving community we hope to support. As more and more teams launch, the need for data will only grow. 

In short: we are laying the foundational data infrastructure to help empower these teams and analyst communities. When complete, on-chain data will enable visibility into growth & usage metrics, both for projects and for Terra 2.0 as a whole. 

In our experience, data insight is a critical cornerstone both for new users and _especially_ for potential investors. We understand if you disagree, and trust in the Terra 2.0 community to determine the importance and merit of analytics & community empowerment.
