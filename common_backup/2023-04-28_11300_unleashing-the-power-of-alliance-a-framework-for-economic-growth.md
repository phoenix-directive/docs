**It is time for a change! Let’s make Terra great again!**

The crash hurt a lot of us and some turned away entirely; this is one way we can help ensure that if/when they do return; we can demonstrate that as an ecosystem; we still have their backs.

I am posting this as an individual, as a long-term luna holder, and someone that spends all their time thinking about how to make Terra great again.

This proposal is a fundamental recalibration of the Terra economy through incentive re-alignment using Alliance.

I have done my best to be neutral and balanced and despite working as founder of one LST provider (ERIS), I hope that this proposal or some improved version of it will be seen to favor no other LSD or other asset or project over another.

The goal of this document is to propose a framework to guide the Terra community in the important matter of selecting Alliance assets.

# Motivation
Right now the staking rewards on Terra are not aligned with the economic growth of the Terra chain. We need to attract users, capital and protocols and we do have the perfect tool now to reallocate rewards for this goal: **Alliance Module**

By using the **Alliance Module **we can build Alliances with projects and economic value drivers on Terra and shift rewards from inactive or non-aligned capital to active capital with vested interest. In return the inactive wallets will receive part of the economy itself.

As Terra is one of the most decentralized chains due to the initial airdrop it has the issue that many of the staking rewards are being distributed to inactive wallets or creating sell pressure due to inactive wallets staking with misaligned validators since genesis.

**Alliance Module **is a perfect fit to realign incentives with the growth of the Terra chain and make it seen in the Cosmos.

With it, we can distribute a part of the inflation and provide it to LP token holders of communities and chains we want to attract. As Alliance is new, there is no inactive capital staked and the active community, validators and the ecosystem will benefit the most.

It will be hard to decide as a community which projects and what liquidity will drive economic growth and a lot of discussion around the possible Alliance parameters, so we want to start with the simplest economic driver alignment: **LUNA Liquid Staking Tokens (LSTs)**

LUNA LSTs enable the most efficient capital use of LUNA across chains and therefore enable increased utility and demand for both, on Terra and also on other Cosmos chains. More integrations allow users to execute more advanced DeFi strategies and increase demand for the token increasing economic activity around Terra and connected chains. Deep liquidity is required in LUNA-[LST] pairs to provide the best user experience, improve integration possibilities and stop cascading liquidations from happening.

**Note; to be clear**; this is not about removing rewards from inactive wallets; so much as adjusting how they receive their rewards such that everyone benefits
- inactive wallet accrues more yield
- LP liquidity grows over time
- Terra gains and strengthens economic security

# Proposal
Start building Alliances with liquid staked LUNA-[LST] LP tokens.

Top-tier lending protocols require around **10 M$ of liquidity** in LST-XYK pools to evaluate listing an asset. To have a buffer for this liquidity a **target of 14 M$** should be used.

Based on the Astroport incentives history a **target rate of 20% APR** in LUNA-[LST] pairs should be the goal.

That means a distribution of 2.8 M$ worth of incentives per year ($ 7,671 per day) paid to active community members and participants in providing stability and economic growth to Terra per LST that fulfills all categories of the framework with a perfect score.

The current stats show that we are spending 250,000 LUNA @ $ 1.25 = $ 312,500 per day on inflation rewards. (see [https://terra.stakebin.io/terra/stats](https://terra.stakebin.io/terra/stats))

To calculate the reward rate we will require a reward percentage of $ 7,671 / $ 312,500 = ~ 2.5 % of the total inflation to reach the goal incentives and liquidity.

In the framework below, there is a total of 16 points reachable. (Bonus points excluded)
If a protocol reaches the full points an Alliance with a reward percentage of 2.5% of inflation or reward rate of 2.5641 should be set. 

**(reached points + bonus points) / 16 * 2.5641 = reward rate **for the LUNA-[LST] LP asset.

The take rate of any LP asset used will be 5 %, which means existing stakers will start compounding LUNA-[LST] LP assets and build up liquidity in the Terra ecosystem. Coming back to the initial motivation, inactive wallets will start accumulating LP tokens which essentially means that Terra is building up **chain owned liquidity**, which is positive for the ecosystem.

To further boost this, a separate proposal in a later stage can be submitted to stake a part of the community pool, so that also the community pool builds up LP tokens.

This proposal will be submitted as a text proposal to the chain to accept the framework, LUNA LST Alliance requests must adhere to.

## Framework
Every protocol and liquid staking provider will have their own definition of what a fair Alliance means. Each LST protocol is differently aligned to the economic growth of the Terra ecosystem and has its unique benefits and drawbacks. A uniform distribution with the same amount of reward rate therefore will not align incentives in the right way.

This is why I am proposing to create a LUNA-LST Alliance framework based on a point system on different categories that are aligned to Terra’s goal to grow the user base and liquidity on the chain.

**Explanations**
- **LST**: Liquid Staking Token
- **Token**: A token means both a coin and a NFT
- **Referenced liquid staking risks**: [https://forum.cosmos.network/t/signaling-proposal-draft-add-liquid-staking-module-to-the-cosmos-hub/10368](https://forum.cosmos.network/t/signaling-proposal-draft-add-liquid-staking-module-to-the-cosmos-hub/10368)


**1. Proxy governance**
- No governance participation: 0 points
- External token used as governance: 1 point
- Users decide governance participation: 2 points

(_Governance risk_)

**2. Validator selection / Delegation criteria**
- Closed validator set: 0 points
- Open validator set: 1 point
- Users decide validator delegations: 2 points

(_validator corruption risk_ and _Vote power concentration risk)_

**3. Alignment**
- Own chain: 0 points
- Own chain with LUNA or LUNA LST as an alliance asset: 1 point
- Building on Terra: 1 point
- Building multiple products on Terra: 2 points


**3. a) Bonus**: 
- 1 point if the project received a grant from the Terra community for an audit or funding. (This shows the community has a vested interest in the projects success - excluding emergency allocations)
- 1 point if the project has no token and building on Terra.
- 1 point if the project is 6 months live and 2 points if the project is 12 months or more live


**4. Liquid staking provider fees**
- higher than 10%: exclusion criteria
- 10% fee: 0 points
- 5% fee: 1 point
- 1% or less fees: 2 points


**5. Revenue Sharing**
- No revenue share with Terra Community Pool: 0 points
- Sharing revenue with Terra Community, e.g. through an NFT minted on Terra: 1 point
- Sharing 5% or more of the revenue with CP: 2 points


**6. Integrations**
- No off-chain integrations: 0 points
- Integration with a Terra Lending Protocol: 1 point
- Integration with a Terra and a Cosmos Lending Protocol: 2 points


**7. Risk**
- No audit: exclusion criteria
- 1 audit: 1 point
- 2+ auditing firms: 2 point


**8. Security**
- Single admin wallet: exclusion criteria
- Multi-sig admin wallet (with builders / community members): 1 point
- Decentralized voting required to upgrade contracts: 2 points


**Fine Print**
- Any change in a category of the framework must be published in the Terra Commonwealth forum and follow up an on-chain vote to increase or reduce received reward rate.


- Teams can only list a single of their LUNA LST (No sybils allowed)


- The LST must have a minimum of $ 50,000 TVL in their staking contracts / chain


- Each community of the LST can decide via their own governance / community process which DEX is being used.


- Each LST must write their own proposal after the passing of the Framework to get listed as Alliance asset and propose their points.


- A minimum of 2 weeks delay time must be used when creating an Alliance asset to allow liquidity to flow in and to not allow frontrunning rewards.


- A max total of 4 liquid staking providers can be active. That means a total of ~10% of LUNA’s inflation can be used for LSTs. If the 4 places are full, the new provider must reach a better score (reached points + bonus points) to be incentivized and the dropped out must be removed.


- LSTs must adhere to the framework when wanting to list LUNA LST assets in any form.


**Notes**
- Additional Alliances will always reduce the existing Alliances reward percentage of the inflation. That means that over time with more Alliances the reward rate of LUNA-LST LP assets will drop as new Alliances are formed.


# Follow-Up Topics after the Proposal
Only native coins can be staked in the Alliance Module. Currently all DEXes on Terra use CW20 LP tokens however.

There are two ways to achieve that:

1. (Optional addition) Wrap any CW20-LP token to a TokenFactory token by providing a simple wrapper contract that can be used by any DEX. Then Alliance GUI’s like from TFL, ERIS or other projects can use that contract to wrap tokens before staking into Alliance Module. And users can unwrap after unbonding.


1. (Recommendation) Develop a DEX pool that can be used by DEXes that is using TokenFactory. Ideally use a Meta Stable pool that is optimized for LUNA-[LST] tokens. Match the $ 7,500 of the Astroport Bounty of developing a Metastable Pool Type, for a TokenFactory based version: [https://forum.astroport.fi/t/arc-56-fund-bounty-to-finalize-metastable-pool-type/1043](https://forum.astroport.fi/t/arc-56-fund-bounty-to-finalize-metastable-pool-type/1043) (will be posted as a separate proposal)
