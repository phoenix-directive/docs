# Introduction

This _Community Spend_ proposal is presented by the **[Momentum One](https://momentum-one.io)** team, who is behind the development of **[Hermes Protocol](https://hermesprotocol.io)**, and other popular projects (such as the **[Sledgehammer](https://docs.sledgehammer.app) **Discord bot). If you are unfamiliar with any of these names, kindly take a look at [this introductory post](https://agora.terra.money/discussion/5933-introducing-momentum-one-hermes-protocol) that goes over both companies' history and track records. It contains important background context and its own FAQ as well.

Both companies were not included in the [Emergency Allocation previously distributed](https://agora.terra.money/discussion/5332-proposed-distribution-method-for-05-emergency-luna-allocation-version-3), despite being present in discussions and actively participating and contributing to the resolution of how those funds should be distributed (and the chain should be restored to a functional state), until the very previous day when a form was sent and the team failed to notice the Telegram group where it was posted.

# Goals

As we believe to have earned our place, as builders, within the community, we are henceforth putting a proposal to receive a grant with the following objectives and next steps:
- Launch **Hermes Protocol’s** web app (initial Beta version) that has been under full development for over half a year; note: Discord bot has already been live for a few months;
- Finish doing much-needed updates to popular Discord bots such as **Sledgehammer** which is protecting the entire Discord community on **Terra** (& more);
- Continue helping Web3 projects which have a sub-par Discord user experience bringing their communities to their best in terms of security, UX and Bot Development;
- Prepare more grant proposals so that we continue moving forward:
- **Hermes Protocol** becoming multi-chain; a lot of Layer-1’s have shown interest in “poaching” our project but we think being cross-chain will benefit much more the community instead of single;
- Building more useful Discord bots and keep improving the existent ones so that the entire ecosystem can benefit from these, rather than a single dApp project;
- Reschedule calls with Angels and VCs after the launch of the web app, so the project can be well funded without having to worry about the runway every month.


# Proposed Grant allocation
- **Total Distribution**/spend: 60,000 (60k) Luna;
- **Vesting Terms**: Equal to the emergency allocation previously disbursed to the other teams: 50% given after approval, and 50% linear vesting for 3 months;
- **Conditions:** The funds will be managed through a vesting smart contract built by TFL and controlled by the emergency council so that it can stop the vesting should the team fail to fulfil the goals that are being set (launch the web app on Terra 2.0 within the next 1-2 months);


Most emergency allocations were given for 3-4 months of runway, to give projects enough time to either survive a while longer, enough to launch on **Terra 2.0** and find a way of sustaining themselves through fees, etc., or find new VC investments.

As **Momentum One**, and subsequently, **Hermes Protocol** as well, has 16 full-time employees based in Portugal, our burn rate isn’t super high and part of it can be subsidised by the services we provide. This grant allocation will give us enough breathing room to fulfil our **Goals** stated in the section above this one.

# FAQ

**Q: Why 60,000 (60k) $LUNA?**

**A:** Right now, we have a burn rate of 40k$ a month with our 16 people structure, an office and all the bills involved. With this amount of $LUNA, we will be able to launch the project that we developed throughout the last 8 months and bring some short-to-medium-term financial stability to **Momentum One**, which was financing and developing **Hermes Protocol**.
With the **Terra Classic** crash, we not only lost the investors' interest but also a very significant slice of the financial reserve of our company.


**Q: How are 16 people working for both companies, yet Hermes Protocol has not been launched after 1-year since its announcement?**

**A: Hermes Protocol **had a POC in August 2021 (last year) and we were a company of 3 people back then. **Hermes Protocol** was a project produced during our free time. Only in November, did **Momentum One** start to grow and be capable of hiring people to develop **Hermes Protocol**. Later in January/February 2022, we managed to assemble the whole team needed for **Hermes Protocol **development.
We continued to invest our resources in the community and have developed **Sledgehammer** and also the **Hermes Protocol** Discord bot for free to answer community needs. And that’s still the plan.
A non-exhaustive list of our staff members can be found on the [**Hermes Protocol **landing page](https://hermesprotocol.io/#OurTeam) and our [**Discord** mini-site’s landing page](https://discord.momentum-one.io/#Team) as well.

**Q: Why ask for a grant for both companies since Momentum One is a for-profit company whose product is not natively Web3?**

**A: Momentum One** is a “web2 company” but it was born within web3. Our clients are 98% web3 and the vast majority of them were located on **Terra Classic**. Due to that fact, we had the majority of our savings there and not split on other blockchains. Our entire team are “Lunatics” and as the **Terra** ecosystem grew exponentially during 2021, we also grew from 3 to 16 people synchronously just to be able to support all the projects that were being built on **Terra**.


**Q: Are there any videos where we can see what has already been built/developed?**

**A: **Yes! We will publish them shortly (1-2 days at most) after this post on [our YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UC7vn0P_TXvCzkBC6fXduALQ), and before the proposal goes live on-chain.
