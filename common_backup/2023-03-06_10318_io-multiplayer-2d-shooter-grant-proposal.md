## Summary
This grant proposal aims to develop a "io" multiplayer 2D shooter game on the Terra blockchain. 
The game will be a 2D browser-based alternative to popular games like Call of Duty and PUBG, 
offering a fast-paced time killer experience with unique in-game incentives. 
With just one click, players can start playing without prior blockchain experience or the need to register via email or social media.
Users will be able to earn or purchase NFTs, such as body skins, weapon skins, and perks, to enhance their gaming experience.

## Reasons for Development
1. The "1 click to play" feature will make the game accessible and user-friendly for web2 users.
2. The Terra blockchain has a limited selection of games.
3. The 2D shooter genre has less competition compared to 3D browser-based games, making it easier to attract a player base.

## Value for Terra
1. The game is expected to attract new web2 users to the Terra ecosystem, leading to an increase in TVL and volume for Terra and its protocols.
2. Terra community will receive 10% of the game's revenue.
3. The addition of games to the Terra ecosystem will make it more comprehensive.
4. Implementing NFTs as in-game assets will provide additional incentives for users to engage with NFT technology within the Terra ecosystem.

## Incentives with Web3 Technology
The game will have a seasonal model where players can earn limited NFTs and other rewards by connecting their wallet. These NFTs can be traded on the market.

## Who We Are

## Funding Request
The project requests funding of 41k USD in LUNA (26797 LUNA as of 03/06/23) for the following expenses:
- Development and coding:                        30k
- Assets (graphics, design, and sound):    5k
- Marketing:                                                       5k
- Server infrastructure:                                   1k (including multiple VPS in North America, Europe, South America, and Asia)
