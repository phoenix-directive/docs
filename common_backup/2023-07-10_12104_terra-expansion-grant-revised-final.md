## **Summary**

The Terra Expansion Grant aims to: 

- Increase Terraform Labs’ (TFL) competitiveness as a Layer 1 (L1) Blockchain Foundation by strengthening its resources
- Fund strategic partnerships and third-party development teams that create additional LUNA utility and further propel the growth of the Terra ecosystem
- Enhance TFL’s capacity to attract and retain top-tier Web3 talent through LUNA token incentives


By doing so, the Terra community will position TFL to steward Terra’s growth alongside them, giving rise to numerous tools, protocols, partnerships, and assets that further boost LUNA’s utility and foster its widespread utilization across Web3.

As we’ll explain in the following section, our vision for Terra extends beyond its use simply as a general-purpose L1 blockchain. Instead, we see it as a network of tools and protocols that empower the next generation of decentralized economies and organizations to flourish – **all fueled by LUNA**.

To help further this vision, TFL will focus on continuing to build out Feather, Alliance, Station, Warp, Enterprise, and other projects, in addition to facilitating strategic partnerships and the administration of community grants to teams that create additional LUNA utility and further catalyze Terra ecosystem growth (more on this later).

TFL is proposing a request of 100M LUNA from the Terra Community Pool to be vested block-by-block linearly over 5 years (enforced programmatically via smart contract). To avoid eroding LUNA staking yield for existing ecosystem participants or giving TFL outsized voting power, none of the LUNA requested will be staked. This request positions TFL to be competitive with other L1 Foundations, eliminates large token unlock events, and ensures TFL can deliver for the Terra community for years to come. We anticipate roughly 30% of the grant to be used to form strategic partnerships and fund third-party teams that develop compelling Terra DeFi projects. However, upon this proposal’s passing, we shall retain flexibility in distributing the funds received in the most beneficial way for the Terra ecosystem. While the uncertainty of the current regulatory environment for cryptocurrency has led TFL to decide not to focus on developing user-facing DeFi apps at this time, we plan to seed third-party teams with ideas/projects for DeFi apps for development and release on Terra (e.g., Nebula, Story, etc.).

## **Decentralized Economies and Their Jump to Universality**

Terra was created on the core conviction that the next evolution of the internet would be built on robust, digital-native economies, inherently both **permissionless** and **decentralized**, and that these economies would have decentralized money. Unfortunately, Web3 has mostly failed to deliver on these promises due to:

- **Poor UX** – the UX for crypto-native products limits its benefits to only the most technical and committed users
- **User/liquidity fragmentation** –  crypto wallet experiences across chains are fragmented and require manual migration of assets
- **High cost of engineering** – there exists a significant cost to launching a new blockchain ecosystem due to the need for coordination of validators / gathering gentxs and generating the genfile / coordinating a genesis event and upgrades, as well as a large set of basic infrastructure tools such as a scalable RPC service, wallet, block explorer, and developer tooling
- **High cost to bootstrap economic viability **– nascent networks typically have a hard time attracting users, liquidity, and developers due to the disjunctive nature of existing blockchain ecosystems and the inability to align incentives effectively


Fortunately, multiple factors uniquely position Terra to address these challenges, including:

- TFL’s experience having developed an entire end-to-end Web3 experience, inclusive of a blockchain, wallet, block explorer, RPC solution, set of apps, and a suite of developer tools
- Terra’s trademark design, which is both simple and intuitive
- Station’s ability to abstract away the complexity of interacting with multiple networks
- Feather’s ability to automate blockchain genesis and offer essential ecosystem tools
- Alliance’s power to bootstrap and strengthen economic viability on both nascent and developed chains
- Warp Protocol’s capacity to create compelling new Web3 platform experiences via decentralized, cost-efficient automation
- Enterprise Protocol’s no-code DAO management platform, which enables organizations built within blockchain ecosystems to thrive
- And much more…


By solving these problems, we think it will become more attractive to launch a blockchain vs. a set of smart contracts for various use cases, in addition to apps built on top of these economies becoming vastly more accessible to the everyday consumer. Making blockchains (and the applications built on top of them) more prolific and accessible in these ways is a prerequisite to enabling decentralized money, as one first needs a robust foundation to build on. Terra’s vision that **decentralized economies need decentralized money** remains the same, and our current work is directed at making decentralized economies more robust.

Our priority for Terra in the near term is to expand to become more than just a general-purpose L1 blockchain. Instead, we see it as a network of tools that empower the next generation of decentralized economies and organizations to flourish – **all fueled by LUNA**.

**Feather 🪶 – Making Blockchain Creation as Light as a Feather**

_Status: Set to arrive in Q3 2023_

Pulling together many of the projects TFL is working on, Feather aims to abstract away the complexity and cost of creating a new blockchain by automating chain genesis and offering essential ecosystem tools, such as an interchain wallet (Station), block explorer, RPC endpoint solution for apps to connect to (Mantlemint), and developer tooling to support new projects (Terrain v2). 

Using Feather, developers can create and deploy a new, customized blockchain in minutes, complete with a full suite of validators and instant tooling parity with larger chains.

The Alliance module will also come pre-installed on every Feather chain, enabling communities to immediately align their monetary policy (i.e., staking rewards) with various objectives that strengthen their ecosystem. LUNA will be able to be used as a fee token on all Feather chains, and those spinning up chains using Feather will also have the opportunity to form an Alliance with LUNA early on to mutually benefit from shared economic value and users.

Feather resolves a variety of problems for developers, including:

- The high initial cost and coordination with validators necessary to create and deploy a new chain
- The difficulty and time required to achieve ecosystem tooling parity with larger chains
- The challenges in scaling as an application on an existing chain


We’re excited to be building what we believe to be state-of-the-art technology in blockchain creation and deployment, and we look forward to sharing more on Feather v1 later this year.

**Alliance 🤝 – Yield Farming for L1s**

_Status: Live; new version set to arrive in Q3 2023_

As decentralized economies evolve to become tenable homes to billions of users worldwide, economic agreements between these digital nation-states will become increasingly more interesting. 

Enter **[Alliance](https://docs.alliance.terra.money/)**. 

Alliance is an open-source Cosmos SDK module that leverages interchain staking to form economic alliances between blockchains. This is accomplished by enabling chains to whitelist almost any token as an Alliance asset, allowing users to stake it and earn a portion of native staking rewards.

Alliance is akin to globalization in the language of nations, enabling free trade agreements between chains. Unlike Mesh or Interchain Security, where validators secure multiple networks and coins are moved to the chain providing the security, **Alliance is not aimed at selling security**. Rather, Alliance enables chains to buy _immigration_: Liquid Staking Tokens (LSTs) are moved from a larger, more liquid chain to a consumer chain, and the cash flows from the LSTs cycle domestically, along with new users and assets coming with it. Cash flows from other ecosystems can be used to augment and diversify the staking yield for the native coin – making it a more interesting asset to hold and stake – and holders of foreign coins have more avenues to boost their yield. Alliance is thus not meant to enable consumer chains to buy security and is very much an economic agreement. If you look historically at why chains fail, it is rarely due to an attack and almost entirely due to the underlying token lacking economic viability.

By enabling almost any token to be whitelisted as an Alliance asset through governance, Alliance can also serve as a general economic tool to steer a chain’s monetary policy (i.e., staking rewards) toward achieving desired objectives, such as:

- Deepening liquidity for essential token pairs by whitelisting LP tokens as Alliance assets
- Incentivizing native application development by whitelisting app or team tokens as Alliance assets


With Alliance now live on Terra, LUNAtics can unleash their full creativity. To set the stage for this new chapter, TFL recently put forth an **[Alliance proposal](https://agora.terra.money/discussion/11500-alliance-signaling-proposal)** to further incentivize the formation of LUNA alliances with other Cosmos ecosystems. We expect the protocol outlined in this proposal to go live, with reward distributions for qualifying tokens commencing via Station, within a month.

The dynamic nature of Alliance enables the Terra ecosystem to thrive both now and into the future. By giving rise to Alliances with numerous chains, a more robust ecosystem of applications, deeper liquidity for essential token pairs, a more diverse community of users, and higher TVL, Alliance sets Terra on a trajectory for tremendous growth. As the number of alliances between Terra and other networks grows through bilateral adoption, causing more LUNA to be staked to earn additional yield, the available supply of LUNA will diminish. In turn, LUNA will become a more attractive coin to whitelist as an Alliance asset on other chains, leading to more chains forming alliances with LUNA, furthering this cycle. Having LUNA LSTs be used for staking across the Cosmos is similar to forming many economic agreements in the language of nations. Over time, the goal should be for LUNA to become one of the most widely used assets across the Cosmos.

**Warp 🌀 – The Decentralized Automation Layer for the Cosmos Ecosystem**

_Status: Live; set to arrive on a variety of Cosmos chains in the coming weeks and months_
_Follow_ **[_@w_arp_protocol](https://twitter.com/warp_protocol) **_on Twitter for updates_

**[Warp Protocol](https://warp.money/) **enables developers to optimize costs and create novel Web3 user experiences via decentralized, cost-efficient automation. Designed to accommodate the limitless potential of a developer’s imagination, Warp enables users to queue _any_ transaction to be executed by keepers in the future based on _any_ available on-chain data. The transactions are called ‘jobs,’ and the circumstances under which they become executable are called ‘conditions.’ When conditions are met, a job can be executed by anyone running a Warp keeper bot in exchange for that job’s reward (paid in the chain's native token). The protocol applies a 5% protocol fee to the total job reward upon job creation. All Warp protocol fees are subsequently automatically converted to LUNA and burned. 

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/d68tyHjjmMCwEUs9nn2kbCml-zxEeK_uMCxM5MDLZlDUjXpgFzWIn1mRZGrIpoxd0ZGVCWfGgwGJ-xuJjBNoy0sx0f8Bd-drd3zw7o1ld0E3XBjWlXxC5PYCKxHHFxjEVHdg6oSMDZIO2c35Kbb9AMY)

Warp is not purpose-built as a user-facing application but rather similar to a programming language, bringing developer’s ideas to life in the form of new platform features. Notice how one can modify, cancel, and view orders (Warp jobs) with different statuses (Open, Closed, Executed).

By eliminating unnecessary complexity often associated with Web3 organizational management, Enterprise empowers communities, like PixeLions and Skeleton Punks, with easy-to-use tools and features to unite and grow their communities. 

Currently serving our community here on Terra, Enterprise has recently set plans for cross-chain expansion in motion. This strategic development underscores the escalating demand we’ve seen from teams across various chains awaiting the opportunity to integrate Enterprise into their ecosystems. As Enterprise deploys across multiple chains, all DAO staking, voting, and funds distribution modules will stay on Terra. Effectively, this means that every vote, every transaction, and every DAO asset staked across any of the chains that Enterprise deploys on will remain on Terra. This allows Terra to capture activity and asset paths while allowing DAOs to have complete control over their assets, regardless of the chain they’re on.

## **Terra Expansion Grant Details**

Since Genesis, TFL has remained an unwavering cornerstone for Terra, leveraging the full weight of our team’s resources for imperative software maintenance and upgrades, the creation and maintenance of developer and essential ecosystem tooling, and the development of useful tools and protocols such as Station, Feather, Alliance, Warp, Enterprise, and others that drive utility to LUNA. 

Funds from the Terra Expansion Grant aim to:

- Increase Terraform Labs’ (TFL) competitiveness as an L1 Blockchain Foundation by strengthening its resources
