Recent efforts have been made to push for LUNA liquidity on Osmosis. This makes a lot of sense in order to get LUNA exposure throughout the broader Cosmos and given there is very little LUNA liquidity currently on Osmosis. TFL was planning a liquidity injection to pair LUNA with OSMO however the effort was halted due to the chapter 11 filing. That being said, there is still a way to obtain such a pool and get deep Osmosis liquidity... through Sail DAO and the Terra CP. 

 Sail DAO is a new liquidity DAO on Osmosis. It is a joint venture between White Whale and Osmosis teams/communities. The Sail DAO treasury has been seeded with 3.5 million OSMO and 10 million WHALE for the purpose of deploying and managing liquidity on Osmosis, particularly on White Whale's new Osmosis DEX outpost. For those wondering why the liquidity would go on WW DEX and not Osmosis main DEX, it really makes no difference as the pools are routed straight through the Osmosis DEX, so anyone can trade the same pool liquidity through either the WW or Osmosis DEX. That being said, here is the proposal: 

1. Sail DAO introduces a spend prop on Terra for 1.5 million LUNA to the Sail DAO treasury
2. Sail DAO airdrops 100 mil SAIL (10% of total SAIL supply) to LUNA voters on the spend prop (no matter how they vote, so long as they participate in the vote)
3. If the prop passes, Sail DAO matches the LUNA with OSMO and deploys the liquidity into a LUNA-OSMO pool on WW's Osmosis DEX
4. The LUNA-OSMO lp lives in the Sail DAO treasury perpertually and can only be moved through a Sail DAO gov prop
5. The LUNA community has a stake in Sail DAO governance given the 10% airdrop


The LUNA community gets deep liquidity on Osmosis and an airdrop, the OSMO community gets LUNA liquidity and volume, the WHALE community gets TVL and volume on their new DEX, and the SAIL community gets LUNA-OSMO lp.
