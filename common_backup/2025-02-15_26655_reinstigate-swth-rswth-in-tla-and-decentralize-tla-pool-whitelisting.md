**Just recently, the Phoenix Directive has made the decision the remove the SWTH-rSWTH pool from the Liquidity Alliance. This text proposal outlines the arguments for why it should be reincluded (for now):**

(Edit: If you like the idea of using the governance process for making changes to which pools can be voted on the Terra Liquidity Alliance, but still want Carbon removed, you should still vote yes and then make a future proposal to remove Carbon. Because this proposal is about ensuring that pools cannot be removed without the Terra governance system. Phoenix Directive can continue all their other operations without problems.)

 
```
1) Carbon/Demex is a valuable partner to Terra because they provide LUNA - USDC and other perpetuals, something that Terra does not have nor will be able to simulate in any meaningful way without wasting vast amounts of valuable resources that could be better spend elsewhere. Let Carbon do what Carbon does best and Terra do what Terra does best. There is no competition here on the product delivery, simply mutual benefit. On top of this, they also provide an excellent platform (Nitron) for borrowing/lending Luna. So they do provide value to Terra even if not on the Terra chain itself. By keeping them in the liquidity alliance, we ensure that this partner will not look elsewhere for liquidity. They need Terra and we need them.
```


```
2) The argument that was made for this decision was that Carbon was simply feeding on Terra while offering near nothing in return. However, they simply used the TLA as intended by design and showcased the bribe system. Meaning that they identified that bribes were at this time currently more effective for growing their pools by attracting voters who locked Luna, rather than voting with Luna themselves. They were paying voters to choose for their pools and therefore should not be punished. If people are punished for using bribes, then what is the point of the bribe system? This was in fact a mutual benefit. A correct usage of the product as designed by ERIS. Working as intended.
```


```
3) The fact that PD can simply decide to remove pools at will reduces trust in the system and creates an atmosphere of fear of punishment rather than inclusion. Ideally, whitelisting and de-whitelisting pools should be decided by collective governance rather than a single entity, even if their intentions are in the right place. I believe chain proposals should always be done in beforehand before adding or removing pools available for vote. Right now, this can be easily done with text proposals (followed by PD acting out the outcome) while in the future ideally should be done automatically through technical proposals (removing middle man altogether). ERIS built a great product and I applaud him for that, however, this does not mean it is already complete. The whole idea of the Liquidity Alliance was to create decentralized liquidity through voting, but it's not truly decentralized liquidity if we can also not vote on the addition/removal of pools. Not working as intended.
```





**Conclusion: Vote Yes on this proposal if you desire whitelisting and de-whitelisting Liquidity Alliance pools to be decided by collective governance rather than a single entity. And to reinstigate Carbon/Demex for now because it was removed without first passing a proposal... Which means that like a chain upgrade, Phoenix Directive or anyone else must first do a proposal before adding or removing pools, so all the Terrans can have their voices heard through voting (democracy).**


Edit: ERIS made a claim saying that if the proposal passes, all the pools that have been added after LA went live have to be removed. WRONG. THIS PROPOSAL IS ABOUT THE POOLS WHICH ARE CURRENTLY LIVE. NO POOLS BE REMOVED. IN ORDER FOR A POOL THAT IS LIVE TO BE REMOVED (after this proposal passes), A PROPOSAL HAS TO PASS. They are planning to remove more pools without community input. Carbon is simply the first to be punished.
