**Terra Expansion Grant**

_Disclaimer:** **I am an employee of Terraform Labs._

We’ve made significant strides as an ecosystem since Terra’s Genesis in May 2022, but it’s time for Terra to enter its next phase of development – expansion. 

## **Overview**

The Terra Expansion Grant aims to:

- Fund the development of numerous useful tools that drive utility to LUNA
- Encourage further use of LUNA across Web3


To accomplish these objectives, Terraform Labs (TFL) will focus on further building out Feather, Alliance, Station, Warp, Enterprise, and other projects, in addition to facilitating the administration of community grants to teams that create additional utility for LUNA (more on this later.)

Having elected to exclude itself from the Terra Genesis airdrop, TFL intended not to receive any LUNA. Since the drop, we have been identifying legacy wallets that may have received LUNA and are in the process of undelegating and donating these tokens to the Terra Community Pool to ensure TFL does not possess any LUNA. Funds provisioned through the Terra Expansion Grant serve to align TFL and other core Terra contributors with the achievement of the above objectives and the long-term growth of the Terra ecosystem.

## **Decentralized Economies and Their Jump to Universality**

Terra was created on the core conviction that the next evolution of the internet would be built on robust, digital-native economies, inherently **permissionless** and **decentralized**. Unfortunately, Web3 has mostly failed to deliver on these promises due to:

- **Poor UX** – the UX for crypto-native products limits its benefits to only the most technical and committed users
- **User/liquidity fragmentation** –  crypto wallet experiences across chains are fragmented and require manual migration of assets
- **High cost of engineering** – there exists a significant cost to launching a new blockchain ecosystem due to the need for coordination of validators / gathering gentxs and generating the genfile / coordinating a genesis event and upgrades, as well as a large set of basic infrastructure tools such as a scalable RPC service, wallet, block explorer, and developer tooling
- **High cost to bootstrap economic viability** –** **nascent networks typically have a hard time attracting users, liquidity, and developers due to the disjunctive nature of existing blockchain ecosystems and the inability to align incentives effectively


Fortunately, multiple factors uniquely position Terra to address these challenges, including:

- TFL’s experience having developed the entire end-to-end Web3 experience, inclusive of a blockchain, wallet, block explorer, RPC solution, set of apps, and a suite of developer tools
- Terra’s trademark design, which is both simple and intuitive
- Station’s ability to abstract away the complexity of interacting with multiple networks
- Feather’s ability to automate blockchain genesis and offer essential ecosystem tools
- Alliance’s power to bootstrap and strengthen economic viability on both nascent and developed chains
- Warp Protocol’s capacity to create compelling new Web3 platform experiences via decentralized, cost-efficient automation
- Enterprise Protocol’s no-code DAO management platform, which enables organizations built within blockchain ecosystems to thrive
- And much more…


By solving these problems, we think it will become more attractive to launch a blockchain vs. a set of smart contracts for various use cases, in addition to apps built on top of these economies becoming vastly more accessible to the everyday consumer. Our vision is for Terra to expand to become more than just a general-purpose L1 blockchain. Instead, we see it as a network of tools that empower the next generation of decentralized economies and organizations to flourish – all fueled by LUNA.

**Station 🛰️ – The Premier Interface to Web3**

_Status: Live; new version set to arrive in Q4 2023_

Earlier this year, TFL released **[Station](https://station.terra.money/)** – an interchain wallet that simplifies the often complex, cumbersome process of interacting with multiple blockchain networks. With Station, users can easily stake, vote, send and receive tokens, and interact with their favorite apps across all 17 supported Cosmos chains.

Station paves the foundation for the multi-chain future outlined above, providing those that automate chain genesis via Feather (discussed below) with immediate interchain wallet functionality and supplying those chains that form economic alliances with others via Alliance (discussed below) with an easy-to-use, easy-to-integrate wallet to execute cross-chain transactions.

Looking toward the future, we are thrilled for the upcoming release of Station v3. This revamped version takes interchain functionality to a whole new level, showcasing a refined design and an even friendlier user interface. While the detailed exploration of the potential impact this may have on LUNA will be part of our Station v3 announcement later this year, it's clear that the ongoing development and increasing adoption of Station stands to unlock many exciting opportunities. 

Ultimately, our vision for Station is one of continuous evolution. As new chains and features are added over time, we envision Station evolving to become the single place to sign all crypto transactions – the canonical center of interchain transaction execution.

**Feather 🪶 – Making Blockchain Creation as Light as a Feather**

_Status: Set to arrive in Q3 2023_

Pulling together many of the projects TFL is working on, Feather aims to abstract away the complexity and cost of creating a new blockchain by automating chain genesis and offering essential ecosystem tools, such as an interchain wallet (Station), block explorer (Finder), RPC endpoint solution for apps to connect to (Mantlemint), and developer tooling to support new projects (Terrain v2). 

Using Feather, developers can create and deploy a new, customized blockchain in minutes, complete with a full suite of validators and instant tooling parity with larger chains.

The Alliance module will also come pre-installed on every Feather chain, enabling communities to immediately align their monetary policy (i.e., staking rewards) with various objectives that strengthen their ecosystem. LUNA will be able to be used as a fee token on all Feather chains, and those spinning up chains using Feather will also have the opportunity to form an Alliance with LUNA early on to bootstrap the economic viability of the network via inbound LUNA yield + users.

Feather resolves a variety of problems for developers, including:

- The high initial cost and coordination with validators necessary to create and deploy a new chain
- The difficulty and time required to achieve ecosystem tooling parity with larger chains
- The challenges in scaling as an application on an existing chain


We’re excited to be building what we believe to be state-of-the-art technology in blockchain creation and deployment, and we look forward to sharing more on Feather v1 later this year.

_Status: Live; new version set to arrive in Q3 2023_

As decentralized economies evolve to become tenable homes to billions of users worldwide, economic agreements between these digital nation-states will become increasingly more interesting. This is where **[Alliance](https://alliance.terra.money/)** comes in. 

Alliance is an open-source Cosmos SDK module that leverages interchain staking to form economic alliances between blockchains. Alliance is akin to globalization in the language of nations, enabling free trade agreements between chains. 

By allowing users to stake multiple tokens on a single chain – earning native staking rewards – Alliance opens up a range of use cases that strengthen decentralized economies, including:

- Attracting users, liquidity, and developers
- Diversifying and augmenting staking yield
- Deepening liquidity for essential token pairs
- And incentivizing native application development

**Warp 🌀 – The Decentralized Automation Layer for the Cosmos Ecosystem**

_Status: Live; set to arrive on a variety of Cosmos chains in the coming weeks and months_

**[Warp Protocol](https://warp.money/) **enables developers to optimize costs and create novel Web3 user experiences via decentralized, cost-efficient automation. Designed to accommodate the limitless potential of a developer’s imagination, Warp enables users to queue _any_ transaction to be executed by keepers in the future based on _any_ available on-chain data. The transactions are called ‘jobs,’ and the circumstances under which they become executable are called ‘conditions.’ When conditions are met, a job can be executed by anyone running a Warp keeper bot in exchange for that job’s reward (paid in the chain's native token). The protocol applies a 5% protocol fee to the total job reward upon job creation. All Warp protocol fees are automatically converted to LUNA and burned. 

![](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/pmHolRcSA-PVQSMcZOv3GObv4g37Kc634dNxfaEUnCZSpbtMRhEVP5DiQZF4qXYOWrPX_nq5VGvuSkorRF15m4VEDnzuxbJjZMSfKAXPoMAlZCSS0gHyi7SNq9gsPROWo3Cyjdvzjo63EPJL5hktcYo)
