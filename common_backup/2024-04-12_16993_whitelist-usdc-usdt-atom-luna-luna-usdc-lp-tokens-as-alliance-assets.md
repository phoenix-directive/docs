Summary
The idea is to go forward with the idea from TFL to white list 3 pools on aliance one for each   terra dex.

Motivation 
TFL is currently in a challenging position, which restricts their actions. However, the community has the freedom to act and should seize this opportunity. 
Luna as a strong defi heritage that we as a community should help spark again. 
Also we as the luna community shouldn't play favourites so should help all dexs. 
This will allow other teams to build on top of this.  

Proposal 
The proposal involves Alliance whitelisting three liquidity pools (LPS) 

One for each DEX: 
- USDC-USDT on Astroport 
- ATOM-LUNA on White Whale 
- LUNA-USDC or USDT on Ura Dex  

Inflation Consideration 
Regarding the inflation to be provided, it is suggested to start with a 1% allocation for each pool. This initial allocation can then be adjusted based on the actual usage, either increasing or reducing the inflation rate as necessary.
