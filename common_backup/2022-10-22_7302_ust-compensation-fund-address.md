## **Summary**
This proposal aims to open an UST compensation fund address in order to execute an airdrop to small cap holders of UST at the 7th may snapshot.

## **Motivation**
For the sake of the solidarity in Crypto community
For the sake of trust in Terra and blockchain innovation.
For the sake of justice towards retails users of the blockchain.

## **Proposal**

**1.What is an UST compensation fund address?**
The UST compensation fund address ( fno. UCFA) is a Terra wallet that will be used solely to hold assets that will be used to compensate victims of the UST de-peg. The wallet would receive any token to be converted immediately in axlUSDC or axlUSDT. 

**2.How would the UCFA be funded?**
The UCFA would be funded by any way, such as:
1.    Allocation of a portion (0,1%) of the network fees to this wallet (like the burn address but without burning)
2.    LFG allocation. LFG stated that it aims to use remaining funds to compensate UST de-peg victims.
3.    Donations from the community in solidarity with the retails users who lost their savings after the UST de-peg. Vitalik Buterin, Justin Sun and others known members of the blockchain community announced their solidarity with UST collapse. 

**3.Airdrop Eligibility**
This Airdrop would be reserved for UST holders pre-de-peg (7th May)
Snapshot Heights for each Blockchain would correspond to the one used for Prop 986 Terra Phoenix Airdrop for “Pre-attack”. 

Why excluding LUNA holders? 
LUNA price was hyper volatile token. The price went from a few cents in January 2021 to 116$ in April 2022. LUNA holders were expecting that the token price could fall at any time, especially that it was meant to defend UST peg by minting mechanism. 

Why excluding post de-peg holders?
While UST holders pre-de-peg were using the token as a stable coin, ie to keep away from volatility and for saving purposes, purchasing UST during or after the de-peg was mainly done for trading or speculative purposes. Hence, holders of UST who purchased the token after the de-peg cannot claim to be victims of it.

**4.Airdrop cap**
Airdrop token would be a stable coin as the UST was meant to be. It would be axlUSDC or axlUSDT.
The aim of this proposal is to make “retail savings” wallet compensated. Hence, a maximum compensation of 150.000 USD would be proposed of each eligible wallet.
If a wallet was holding for example 10.000 UST at the time of the de-peg start (7th May), it would receive a compensation of 10.000 axlUSDC. 
If a wallet was holding more than 150.000 UST at the time of the depeg start (7th May), it would receive no more than 150.000 axlUSDC. 

**5.UCFA target list amount**
Reading of Terra Blockchain at the time of the de-peg start should show:
1-   Which and how many wallets were holding UST (target list)?
2-   How much UST each wallet was holding ?
3-   How much is the total target amount UCFA should hold to fulfill complete compensation
Target amount = (total holdings of wallet with less than 150.000 UST cap) + (150.000 * number of the wallet with over the cap of UST holding at the time of de-peg)

The detailed list of the wallet to compensate would be publicly declared to follow-up compensation process in transparency. 

**6.Airdrop mechanism**
To ensure transparency and trust, the airdrop mechanism would run automatically by a smart contract to be audited once the target list and amount is published. 

**7.Airdrop deployment and Dashboard**
Airdrop would start with the smallest amount wallets to compensate with any first fund received on UCFA. 
A Dashbaord would show how much and which wallets were compensated, the amounts refunded and still to refund.
