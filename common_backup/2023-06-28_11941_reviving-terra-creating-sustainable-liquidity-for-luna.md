**1/ Context:**
			Currently, $USDC is being used for transactions on Terra. Additionally, there are many other coins like $Solid that 		want to launch on Terra.

**2/ Risks:**
			When using $USDC, users incur a fee percentage.
			When using $Solid, users incur fees and require ampLuna as collateral. If Luna's value decreases, the collateral will be liquidated, causing Luna to depreciate further.

**3/ Proposal**

_**	a. Project Name:**_
		Relaunching $UST 2.0 (referred to as UST)

_**	b. Operation:**_
		 TFL and LFG

_**	c. Payment Promotion Process:**_
_Phase 1:_
	UST will be collateralized with the US dollar, with **1 UST = 1 USD.**

**Operation:**
	Initially, it will apply to the Luna community, TFL products, and projects on Terra.
Transactions will incur a fee similar to $USDC.

**Purpose**:
	To create UST liquidity and generate profits for the project through fees.
	The fees will be used to acquire collateral assets in Phase 2.

_Phase 2: _
	When UST reaches a market cap of around 100M - 200M
	UST will be collateralized with a combination of different currencies:
For example:
	1 UST will be collateralized as follows: 20% Gold + 10% USD + 10% £ + 10% Euro + 10% CAD...
					(9 major global currencies - referred to as the Lion Assurance Fund)
	Value: **1 UST = 1 USD**
Operation similar to Phase 1

_Phase 3:_
	UST when widely circulated in the Universe.
	Market cap reaches 1 billion dollars.

	UST will be collateralized as follows:
	1 UST will be collateralized as follows: 10% Gold + 10% USD + 10% £ + 10% Euro + 10% CAD... + 10% Luna
				(9 major global currencies)

				_Note: 10% of Luna will be burned to generate UST._
Value: **1 UST** = **1 USD**

Operation similar to Phase 1

Key Condition: The Lion Assurance Fund must always be larger than 10% of the total UST supply in order to proceed with burning Luna to generate UST.
For example, if there are 10 billion UST in circulation, a minimum of 500M Luna must be burned.

_**Risk Mitigation:**_
When UST is manipulated and sold extensively, the Lion Assurance Fund will bear the responsibility, and Luna will only bear less than 10%. Thus, the price of Luna will not experience a sudden decrease.
The total value of the Lion Fund must exceed 10% of the total UST supply because transaction fees for UST will be returned to the Lion Assurance Fund.

_**Why not use BTC as collateral:**_
If BTC is used as collateral, when UST is sold off, UST will decrease, leading to a decrease in Luna. When we sell UST and BTC, the price of BTC will decrease, affecting the entire market, resulting in significant domino effects.
If UST is built in this direction, UST and Luna will support each other.
